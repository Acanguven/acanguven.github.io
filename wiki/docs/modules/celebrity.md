# Celebrity module

The **celebrity** pack adds a **fame** path: public attention, star-making jobs, and story beats that treat your character as a **public figure** instead of an anonymous citizen.

## Fame

**Fame** tracks how widely you are known. Higher fame:

- Unlocks **premium jobs**, especially **acting** and performance-related careers
- Ties into **quests and achievements** around spotlight moments
- Changes **social** reactions — people recognize you more often

:::tip
Fame works best alongside **looks** and **performance skills** you can train in the base game — casting and big breaks often check several stats, not fame alone.
:::

## Acting and entertainment

The pack supports **actor / entertainer** style careers:

- Audition-style steps and promotions through the **job** system
- **Story choices** and events around press, scandals, and big breaks
- Money can spike when you **hit it big**, but downside events also show up (public shame, stalker-style beats, and similar)

## Streaming

The **streamer** career lets you build a personal channel and grow an audience:

- **Personalized channel** — choose your channel name and content category (gaming, IRL, music, tech, creative) when you start
- **Subscriber system** — your follower count grows over time and directly affects earnings and viewer counts
- **Stream series** — multi-day events with random encounters like trolls, raids, donations, copyright strikes, and viral moments
- **Gaming PC upgrades** — buy and upgrade a gaming PC at the electronics shop for better streaming performance, up to level 5
- **Mini-game** — the **Go Live** action uses a tap-targets mini-game that gets easier with a better PC

:::info
Streaming and acting are both fame careers but play differently: actors chase auditions and movie releases, streamers build a subscriber base and manage their channel daily.
:::

## How to become famous (strategy)

1. **Unlock** the celebrity pack and take a job that puts you in the **public eye**.
2. Train **relevant skills** (performance, speech, looks where the game asks for them).
3. **Engage** with pack events — repeated positive beats usually raise fame faster than waiting.
4. Reinvest income in **education** or **grooming** if something blocks the next tier.

:::warning
Fame without savings is risky. Bad press can hit **morale** or **money**; early on, keep a normal job in mind as a backup.
:::
