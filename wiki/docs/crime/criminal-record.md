# Criminal record

**Criminal record** is a **persistent counter** separate from day-to-day **wanted** pressure. It summarizes how “heavy” your legal history is across the run.

## When it increases

On **first imprisonment** for a sentence, the game **adds your current remaining sentence** into **criminal record** at the moment you are processed into prison. Longer stacked sentences before capture mean a **bigger permanent jump**.

:::info
**Criminal record** does **not** tick down automatically when you leave prison. It is a **lifetime ledger**, not a second sentence timer.
:::

## How it relates to other crime state

| Concept | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| **Remaining sentence** | Working clock for wanted / prison time; can hit **0** after release or legal reduction |
| **Wanted** | Clears when **remaining sentence** is **0** (outside prison) |
| **In prison** | No longer true after release or escape |
| **Criminal record** | **Accumulates**; long-term stain on your history |

## Gameplay effects (high level)

Exact uses can expand with updates, but design intent is clear:

- **Story weight** — repeat offender fantasy; pairs with achievements like extreme sentences (**100+** days on intake can flag **Public Enemy**-style recognition)
- **Module flavor** — future jobs, loans, or events can read record severity

:::warning
Do not confuse **criminal record** with **Prison Rep**. Rep is **inside-prison** influence and **deactivates** when you leave; record **stays** on the character sheet.
:::

## Player takeaway

If you care about a “clean” legacy character, **avoid stacking crime days** before arrest. If you are min-maxing prison content, expect record to **spike** once you finally go in — plan the rest of your run around being an **ex-con** narratively.
