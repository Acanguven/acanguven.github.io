# How to commit crimes

Illegal actions live in the **crime module** and usually require you to be in the right **location**, have enough **energy**, and pass **skill checks**, **decisions**, or **mini-games**.

## Crime skill

The **crime** skill is the backbone of most illegal actions. Higher skill:

- Improves **success odds** on probability-based crimes
- Unlocks or softens harder variants (e.g. smoother pickpocket runs)
- Stacks with other relevant skills (e.g. **driving** for vehicle theft, **persuasion** where dialogue matters)

:::tip
Train crime through **repeated attempts** and module-specific jobs or actions. Pair it with **fitness** skills if crimes often end in chases or fights.
:::

## Representative crime categories

| Theme | What you are doing | Risk profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Theft** | Pickpocket, shoplift-style flows | Police attention, fines, wanted days |
| **Vehicles** | Steal / joyride cars | High heat; chase decisions if spotted |
| **Documents** | Forgery / fraud-style actions | Skill gates; can spike wanted heavily |
| **Smuggling** | Move contraband | Often multi-step; failure adds crime days |

Exact action names and on-screen text appear in-game; this page describes **systems**, not every button label.

## Success factors

- **Skill level** — directly affects many random chance checks on illegal actions
- **Location & timing** — some crimes only appear at night or in specific areas
- **Status** — low health/morale can block actions or make fights unwinnable
- **Mini-games / decisions** — failures often still apply **partial penalties** (money loss, injury, extra wanted days)

:::warning
Failed crimes usually add **remaining sentence** even before arrest. Stacking wanted pressure before you are caught makes prison stays longer.
:::

## Risk / reward

**Reward:** money, items, quest progress, achievements, and story beats.  
**Risk:** wanted flag, extended sentence, job loss on imprisonment, injuries, and long-term **criminal record** (see [Criminal record](./criminal-record)).

Balance short-term cash against whether you can survive **prison** if caught — see **[Prison life](./prison-life)**.
