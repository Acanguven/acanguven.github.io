# Crime & prison overview

The **Crime** module (purchasable game module) layers risk-and-reward illegal activity on top of the core life sim. In **Life Simulator Reloaded**, crime is not a separate mode: it is woven into locations, skills, decisions, and long-term character state.

## What the crime module adds

| Area | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Street crime** | Pickpocketing, car theft (GTA-style), smuggling, forgery, and related actions tied to the **crime** skill |
| **Consequences** | **Wanted** status, police chases, arrest, sentencing, and **prison** as a full location loop |
| **Prison** | Dedicated locations (cell, yard, gym, library, chapel, etc.), inmate interactions, fights, mini-games, quests, and **Prison Rep** |

:::info
You only experience prison content while you are **actively serving a sentence**. Your **remaining sentence** (wanted pressure on the outside, time left on the inside) drives escape pressure before arrest and how long you serve after capture.
:::

## Typical loop

1. Commit crimes or fail risky checks → you gain **remaining sentence** days and can become **wanted**.
2. While wanted, travel and police interceptors can trigger **chase decisions** — **escape at home** or **escape on the road**, depending on context.
3. Capture sends you to **prison**; your stay is tied to **remaining sentence**, and your **criminal record** grows when you are first imprisoned.

:::warning
Crime scales with skills and luck but always carries **sentence extensions**, **reputation hits**, or **forced fights**. Plan energy and health before long illegal sessions.
:::
