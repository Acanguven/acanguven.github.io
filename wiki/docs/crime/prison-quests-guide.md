# Prison quests walkthrough

Entering prison **auto-starts** all three quests: **Survivor**, **Scholar**, and **Kingpin**. They overlap; order below minimizes friction.

## Recommended order (strategy)

1. **Survivor** — passive; just **stay alive** **10** / **20** / **30** days **served in prison** (tracked as time inside).
2. **Scholar** — actively park in **library** for random finds, **letters**, chapel, counseling — anything that trains **Prison Life** toward **50%** and stacks **books read**.
3. **Kingpin** — needs **3 fight wins**, **gang formed**, and a **very high Prison Rep** milestone (push rep aggressively late; the quest expects top-tier inside influence).

:::info
Survivor milestones tick on **days served in prison**, not sentence length on paper. You can complete Survivor **while** working the other two if you do not early-release.
:::

## Survivor

| Milestone | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Serve 10 / 20 / 30 days | Time served in prison |

**Play pattern:** keep morale healthy (pray, letter, meditate), avoid solitary spirals, and do not riot until you understand the risk.

## Scholar

| Milestone | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Read **5** books | Driven by **library random events** and books-read progress |
| **50%+ Prison Life** | Passive +1/day plus training actions |

**Efficiency:**

- Visit **library** often to roll the **library find** event pool.
- Stack **letter**, **counseling**, **gossip** interactions, and successful **meditate** for extra Prison Life growth.

:::warning
If you join a **gang**, you **lose** parole, counseling, community service, and the passive good-behavior tick — Scholar is still doable, but slower if you needed those crutches.
:::

## Kingpin

| Milestone | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Win **3** fights | Any prison fight win counts |
| **Form gang** | Rep **≥ 80**, **2+** jail friends **≥ 50** friendship, win initiation fight |
| **Rep milestone** | Raise Prison Rep until the quest completes (very high bar — think endgame inside status) |

**Efficiency:**

- Farm **fight training** for skills, then pick **inmate fight** interactions or yard events.
- After gang unlock, **gang meetings** every **2** days spike rep quickly.
- Successful **riot** middle band grants **+25** rep (but riot is **once** and risky).

:::tip
Complete **Scholar books** before going all-in on gang (optional). Kingpin wants **social** investment anyway — align **friendship** grinding with gang requirements.
:::
