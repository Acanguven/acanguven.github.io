# Hospital vs witch house

Both locations address **health crises**, but through different **economy** and **risk** models. Core gives you **science** at the hospital and **occult commerce** at the **witch house**.

## Hospital

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| **See doctor** action diagnoses **all** active diseases at once | Requires **travel** to hospital location |
| Enables **daily cure rolls** per disease after care | Cure is **probabilistic** — may take several days |
| Works in **prison clinic** identical action | Does not instantly wipe diseases |

**Best for:** early/mid game, steady income, role-play “normal” lives, and **prisoners** (clinic access).

:::tip
If you are **danger** (≤20 on a meter), detour to hospital **before** stacking risky actions — doctor flagging is free aside from travel time.
:::

## Witch house

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| **Potion** item can **instantly clear** every disease | **Expensive** — money and/or **gem** listing |
| No waiting for nightly cure RNG | Requires **witch** location access + inventory space |
| Thematic stat / crafting hooks | Less integrated with insurance-like gameplay fantasy |

**Best for:** emergency clears before a **fight**, **job interview** day, or **travel** you cannot delay.

:::warning
Relying on witch **gems** every illness is a **hard-currency sink**. Hospital care is cheaper long-term if you can afford the time.
:::

## Decision cheat sheet

| Situation | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| First disease, stable money | **Hospital** |
| Stacked diseases + low health | **Witch potion** if you can afford it |
| In prison + sick | **Clinic doctor** (same action) |
| Min-max frugality | **Hospital** + sleep/food to stabilize meters |
