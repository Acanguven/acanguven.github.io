# Health system

Your character has three **status meters**: **health**, **energy**, and **morale**. Each has a **current** and **maximum** value; actions, diseases, jobs, and events raise or lower **current**.

## Danger and death

| Rule | What it means in play |
| --- | --- |
| **Danger** | If **any** of the three meters is **20 or below**, you are in **danger** — the game warns you |
| **Death** | If **any** meter hits **0 or below**, your character **dies** |

:::warning
Because **any** meter can kill you, a **morale** crash is as deadly as physical harm. Prison taunts, job-loss spirals, and riot failures can all lead to a “quiet” game over.
:::

## Managing the triangle

| Meter | Typical drains | Typical recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health** | Disease, fights, accidents, bad food | Hospital, rest, some items |
| **Energy** | Work, training, travel | Sleep, food, some events |
| **Morale** | Repeated failure, hate, isolation | Social actions, hobbies, chapel |

:::tip
Before heavy **crime** or **prison** arcs, try to keep **each** meter comfortably above **20** so one bad day does not drop you straight into **danger**.
:::

## Related systems

- **Diseases** — ongoing health drain; see **[Diseases](./diseases)**
- **Healing locations** — **[Hospital vs witch](./hospital-vs-witch)**
- **Revival** — rare story beats can bring you back after a death-like outcome in special cases

Watch **danger** often if you are **wanted** or **hunting** — both loops like to chip away at more than one meter at a time.
