# Locations overview

In **Life Simulator Reloaded**, **locations** are places your character can travel to. Each spot unlocks different **actions** (things you can do that turn), **jobs** (when you are employed there), **shopping** categories, and **random events** that fire when you visit or pass time.

## Two worlds on the map

**City and public areas** — Most of the game happens here: home, parks, shops, school, and services. You can move freely between them unless an action or story state blocks you (for example, prison).

**Prison** — When you are **imprisoned**, the city list is replaced by **jail locations** (yard, dormitory, prison gym, library, infirmary, cafeteria, chapel, workshop, and solitary). Routine, jobs, and fights are covered in **[Crime & Prison: Prison life](../crime/prison-life)**.

:::tip Strategy
Keep a mental “shopping route”: **Market** for general goods and haggling, specialist shops for weapons, electronics, and jewelry, and **Black Market** only when you accept crime risk.
:::

## Module / optional locations

**Hunter's Guild** and **Dark Forest** appear when the **Monster Hunter** content is in play. See **[Modules: Monster Hunter](../modules/monster-hunter)**.

| Prison gym / library | Train like city counterparts |

| Cafeteria, chapel, workshop | Jobs, quests, routines |
