# Home

**Home** is tied to your **current house**. It is your default base for domestic actions, relaxing, and many **childhood** activities. You must actually **have housing** for the location to appear.

## What you can do

- **Cook fish** — Turn **raw fish** into **cooked fish** at home (cooking skill); other meal prep may use items and recipes elsewhere.
- **Garden** — Once per day (when available): click-game tending, **cooking** and **calmness** skill, morale; periodic **herb** harvests and a related achievement.
- **Watch television** — Requires the **TV furniture** in use; pick a genre for morale (and sometimes **intelligence** on documentaries). Cooldown between uses.
- **Early life** — Babies and children get actions such as **babble**, **tantrum**, **nap**, **play with toys**, and similar home-adjacent routines.

## When to visit

Use home to **recover through the day loop** (advance days / rest via your normal play flow), **save money** by cooking caught fish instead of dining out, and **stack calmness + cooking** from gardening before stressful jobs or crime.

:::tip Strategy
Buy and **use the TV furniture** early if you want a cheap, repeatable morale (and occasional int) boost without leaving the house.
:::

## Special events

**Beggar** and other hooks sometimes care whether you are **at home vs. out in the city**, changing odds and logs. **Throwing rocks** (vandalism) is intentionally **not** available at home.
