# Hospital

The **hospital** is the legitimate medical hub in the **city**. It focuses on **disease workflow** and optional **cosmetic surgery** — both cost **money**.

## What you can do

- **See a doctor** — If you have **active diseases**, the doctor **marks them as hospital-cared**, which enables cure chances over time in the disease system. With nothing active, you get a “no disease” style outcome.
- **Plastic surgery** — Adult-only; **exponential price** each time you use it. Large **looks** stat bump on success.

## When to visit

As soon as you suspect **sickness** (symptoms / logs), visit once to **register care** — cures still tick with chance afterward, but doctor visit is the gate. Save **plastic surgery** for late-game money dumps when looks gates block promotions or fame.

:::tip Strategy
Compare with **[Witch house](./witch-house)** — potions can wipe diseases in one use, while hospital is the “system” path tied to ongoing cure rolls.
:::

## Special events

Disease **cure ticks** after care are handled in simulation, not as a single hospital button. **Prison infirmary** mirrors doctor access while locked up.
