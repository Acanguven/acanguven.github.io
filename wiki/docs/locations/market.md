# Market

The **market** is the general **commerce** floor. Expect category shopping (food, gear, and more depending on modules) plus a standout **social** action.

## What you can do

- **Shop the stalls** — Use the standard **buy/sell** flows while standing here; stock rotates with game economy rules and unlocks.
- **Haggle** — Once per day when allowed. **Decision**-based **persuasion** check. **Success**: free **food or misc** item, better table if persuasion is high. **Failure**: morale hit and a **temporary ban** from haggling (tracked in storage) — do not spam carelessly.

## When to visit

Your **default pit stop** whenever you need consumables or to liquidate loot. Add **haggle** on days you want **free inventory** padding and **persuasion** training.

:::tip Strategy
If haggling **bans** you after a bad roll, wait out the cooldown — the game stores a “banned until day” flag.
:::

## Special events

**Black market** and specialist shops split **illegal** or **luxury** stock; market remains the **bread-and-butter** storefront. Random **city** events may mention shopping but rarely replace the core UI.
