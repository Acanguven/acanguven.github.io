# Bar

The **bar** is a **city** nightlife spot. It mixes **money sinks**, **mini-games / decisions**, and **social** payoffs — and it supports **bartender** work (see **[Jobs: Bartender](../jobs/bartender)**).

## What you can do

- **Pub quiz** — Pays an entry fee, then a **decision** check; success returns **cash**, **intelligence**, **communication**, morale, and tracks **quiz wins** toward an achievement. Can **meet a new NPC** sometimes.
- **Karaoke** — Costs a small cover; **click-game** performance. Success boosts **communication** and morale, can add a friend, and awards a **karaoke** achievement.
- **Arm wrestling** — **Decision** challenge vs. a patron; wins stack toward **arm wrestling** achievements.

## When to visit

Spend disposable cash when you want **int + communication** together (quiz), or **morale spikes** (karaoke). Good stop while pursuing **celebrity / social** arcs that care about friends met in the wild.

:::tip Strategy
Space **quiz** attempts — it has a **multi-day cooldown** so you cannot spam it daily for infinite prizes.
:::

## Special events

Bar outcomes sometimes **inject random NPCs** into your relationship list after strong performances. Bartender shifts (job) add separate on-the-clock events not tied to patron actions.
