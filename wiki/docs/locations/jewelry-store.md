# Jewelry store

The **jeweler** handles **high-end** accessories. Shopping lists diamonds and display pieces, while the spotlight action is **professional appraisal**.

## What you can do

- **Appraise items** — Requires carrying **rings**, **coins**, or similar flagged valuables and paying a **fee** on a weekly cadence. **Decision** flow: success grants **intelligence** and morale; owning **fake** rings risks **exposure** (looks + morale crash) or, on luck, **selling the fake** for big money. **Gold coins** add int even before the decision resolves.

## When to visit

After **crime**, **treasure**, or **relationship** gifts leave you unsure what you hold. Also useful before **pawn-style** choices elsewhere so you know real vs. fake.

:::tip Strategy
Never appraise **fakes** unless you accept **failure risk** — success can dump a worthless ring for **500** cash, failure tanks **looks**.
:::

## Special events

The **appraisal decision** bundles the drama; no roaming ghost events. Pair with **[Market](./market)** and **[Black market](./black-market)** for how goods enter inventory.
