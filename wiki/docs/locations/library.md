# Public library

The **public library** is the civilian reading room. The same **read a book** option also appears at the **prison library** when you are locked up — same activity, different place.

## What you can do

- **Read a book** — Choose **any skill** you still have room to improve; a **challenge** simulates turning pages. **Success** gives a large boost to that **skill** plus **intelligence**, clears the book you were working through, and costs **energy**. **Failure** still teaches a little. Prisoners use the same flow to study behind bars.

## When to visit

Best when you want **one skill** to climb off the clock — languages, niche job skills, or shoring up weak spots before **choices** and **job interviews**.

:::tip Strategy
Each skill remembers **how far you are into a book** until you finish — start a session when you have energy so you are not stuck mid-book when you are drained.
:::

## Special events

Nothing unique to the room beyond reading-related **achievements** elsewhere in the game. For jail reading, see **[Prison life](../crime/prison-life)**.
