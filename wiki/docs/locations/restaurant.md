# Restaurant

The **restaurant** is where you **eat out** for instant needs and play a **cooking challenge**. It is also the workplace for **waitress** and **cook** paths (see **[Jobs](../jobs/overview)**).

## What you can do

- **Dine out** — Pick a **meal tier** (street food up to chef's table) if you can afford it; restores **health**, **energy**, and **morale** once per day. The top tier can raise **intelligence**. Small chance to **meet a stranger** (counts toward social achievements).
- **Cooking challenge** — Weekly; **skill challenge** with **cooking** rewards, cash on success, and a chance at a **chef's knife** if your **cooking** is high enough. Failure costs morale and energy.

## When to visit

Use **cheap tiers** when you are hurt or exhausted; use **gourmet** or **chef's table** before exams or checks that care about **intelligence**. Run the **challenge** when you want **money** and **cooking** together.

:::tip Strategy
If you want the **knife**, raise **cooking** first — the bonus item only rolls after a successful challenge, and your **cooking level** matters.
:::

## Special events

**Dates** and **anniversaries** can pick the **restaurant** when it fits, which changes flavor and cost. If you work here, job events stack on top of what you do as a customer.
