# Gym

The **city gym** is the main **paid** training spot for **sports**, **looks**, and **fighting** — separate from the **prison gym** while you are jailed.

## What you can do

- **Workout / lift weights** — Costs **money** each session; **skill challenge** reps. Success trains **sports**, raises **looks**, and spends energy through the activity. Available here **or** at the **prison gym** if you are inside.
- **Boxing match** — Entry fee plus a **timing mini-game** (stop the marker in the green zone). **Win**: cash, **fighting** and **sports**, morale, and wins count toward **knockout**-style achievements. **Lose**: big **health** and morale loss. **Boxing gloves** make the timing window a bit wider.

## When to visit

Prioritize when you are building **fighting** for crime, patrol fights, or brawls, or **looks** for careers and romance. Skip if you are broke — losing a boxing match hurts.

:::tip Strategy
Wear **boxing gloves** before matches; the extra window combines with your **fighting** and **sports** levels for easier timing.
:::

## Special events

No location-only story beyond **achievement** milestones and combat stat growth; prison has its own gym beats in **[Prison life](../crime/prison-life)**.
