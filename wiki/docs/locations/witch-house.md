# Witch house

The **witch house** lives in the **woods** area — a secluded **alternative medicine** vendor with **gem** and **cash** priced **curios**.

## What you can do

- **Shop exclusive stock** — While physically here (or in special cases such as **after death** for select items), buy **potions** that **clear all active diseases** on use, **life stones** that can **revive** you once on death (long buy cooldown after purchase), and other **witch-tagged** gear listed in the market sheet.
- **Arrive** — The location emits a **door / witch** flavor log the first time you step in to sell the fantasy tone.

## When to visit

Emergency **disease wipes** if you cannot wait for **[Hospital](./hospital)** cure ticks, or **hardcore insurance** via **life stone** before dangerous crime/monster content.

:::tip Strategy
Potions are pricey — compare **1000 cash / 15 gems** style costs against a hospital visit if you only need diagnosis + patience.
:::

## Special events

**Life stone** hooks run on **death** (consuming the item). Few overworld traps fire just from standing in the hut — drama is mostly **inventory** based. Compare timing and cost with **[Hospital](./hospital)** disease care when planning builds.
