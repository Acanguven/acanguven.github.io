# Electronics shop

The **electronics shop** sells **phones**, **computers**, and related gear via the market UI, and offers a **repair gig** that pays **scaled cash**.

## What you can do

- **Repair gadgets** — Cooldown-gated. Burns **energy**, then runs a **decision** check. **Success**: random **payout**, **basic computer** + **programming** skill, statistic toward **gadget** achievements, rare **bonus component** money roll. **Failure**: morale hit but minor skill consolation.

## When to visit

Early **money** for teens who lack jobs, or mid-game **skill** bridging before **developer** interviews. Buy **electronics** here when you need **internet**-gated actions elsewhere.

:::tip Strategy
Stack repairs after **sleep / energy** recovery — the flat energy penalty hurts on an already tired character.
:::

## Special events

Rare **bonus payout** log after success; otherwise interactions are self-contained. See **[Items: Electronics](../items/electronics)** for what to buy.
