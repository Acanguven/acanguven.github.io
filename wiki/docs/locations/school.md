# City school

**School** is the daytime education hub for teens and adults. It combines **free actions** with the **Education** screen for paid **courses** and degrees.

## What you can do

- **Study** — Daily **challenge**: big gains to **academics** and **intelligence** on success, partial reward on failure, costs **energy**.
- **Skip class** — Morale buff with an **academics** penalty; you may get caught (worse for academics).
- **Cafeteria hangout** — Once per day **morale** and **communication**.
- **Talk to teacher** — **Academics** and **communication**; sometimes extra mentorship for more academics.
- **Enroll in courses** — Use **Education** while you are here to buy lessons that finish over days (see **[Education](../education/overview)**).

## When to visit

Every day during student arcs to keep **academics** high before **exams** and job options. Combine **cafeteria** and **teacher** when you want **social** growth without going to the **[Bar](./bar)**.

:::tip Strategy
Skipping is tempting for morale — keep **academics** high enough if you rely on scholarships or school-related story beats.
:::

## Special events

While enrolled, random scenes can play out here — cafeteria drama, bullies, and similar moments — separate from the main action buttons. Keep showing up to see them.
