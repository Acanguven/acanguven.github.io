# Airport

The **airport** is a **city** travel hub. Today it centers on **sightseeing flights** rather than a full world-map travel sim — think of it as **premium morale + stat tourism** with a weekly cadence.

## What you can do

- **Sightseeing flight** — Adult-only. Pay for **coastal**, **mountain**, or **city** routes (increasing price). Each route refunds different **morale**, **calmness** or **communication**, and **stat** bumps (**looks** or **intelligence** depending on route). **Energy** cost is baked into the trip. Weekly cooldown between flights. Small chance for a **souvenir** item on completion.

## When to visit

When you are **flush with cash** and need a **big morale reset** plus a permanent **stat** nudge. Less efficient than grinding skills, but fast and flavorful.

:::tip Strategy
If you only afford one flight in a week, compare route **stat** rewards to your current build (e.g., mountain/city skew **int**, coastal skews **looks** + calmness).
:::

## Special events

The **souvenir drop** is the main extra proc; broader **intercontinental travel** mechanics may expand in future updates — the airport UI is the natural hook.
