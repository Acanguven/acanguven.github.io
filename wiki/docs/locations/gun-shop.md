# Gun shop

The **gun shop** sells **weapons** and **ammunition** through the item **market** while you are at this location, and hosts a **shooting lane** mini-game.

## What you can do

- **Target practice** — Costs **money** and **energy** on a cooldown. **Stop-the-bar** timing mini-game: success trains **fighting** and **calmness**, boosts morale. Owning a **rifle** widens the hit window slightly. Tight-window success can award **sharpshooter** achievements.
- **Browse firearms** — Purchase weapons for other systems (crime, hunting modules) when unlocked in the catalog.

## When to visit

If you plan **combat-heavy** arcs, train **fighting** here alongside **[Gym](./gym)** boxing — different skill mix (adds **calmness**). Buy hardware **before** jobs or quests that check gear.

:::tip Strategy
Practice **before** you need fighting checks; failed runs still teach a little fighting but waste cash and mood.
:::

## Special events

No bespoke random queue; achievement thresholds (**sharpshooter**) and **inventory** checks (rifle bonus) are the hidden depth.
