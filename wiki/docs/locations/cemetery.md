# Cemetery

The **cemetery** is a quiet **city** location built around a single powerful daily action: **prayer**. Outcomes are **randomized** but always interact with **calmness**, and often **morale** or **stats**.

## What you can do

- **Pray** — Once per game day. Costs a little **energy**, trains **calmness**, and rolls among outcomes: peaceful morale boost, deep reflection (+ **intelligence**), **finding coins** on a grave, **eerie wind** (mixed morale but big calmness/honor), or **ghostly whispers** (scary: morale/health hit but huge calmness growth). Repeated visits count toward a **cemetery devotee** achievement.

## When to visit

Best on **low-morale** days when you can accept variance, or when training **calmness** for decisions and stress events. Avoid if you are already injured and cannot risk the rare **health** penalty branch.

:::tip Strategy
Treat prayer as a **calmness engine** first; the **coin** branch is a nice bonus, not the main reason to go.
:::

## Special events

The **prayer** roll *is* the event bundle — no separate hidden queue. Some **items** (for example **holy water** hooks in content) can interact with cemetery logic in other systems; watch logs when experimenting.
