# Park

The **park** is an outdoor **city** location focused on **health**, **sports**, **social skills**, and light **money** opportunities.

## What you can do

- **Collect flowers** — Nature gather action while you are at the park (ages 4+).
- **Playground** — Young children play here for morale, sports skill, and tutorial quest progress.
- **Jog** — Daily click-game: **energy** cost, **health** and **looks** upside, **sports** skill; chance to **find a wallet** (return for honor/morale or keep for cash + evil skill). **Binoculars** improve find odds. Long-term jogs unlock a **jogger** achievement.
- **Busking / street performance** — Decision-based tips; can meet NPCs and rarely a **talent scout** bonus payout.

## When to visit

Train **sports** and **looks** cheaply, farm **honor** (return wallet) or **evil** (keep wallet), and grind **communication** + cash early as a teen/adult.

:::tip Strategy
Bring **binoculars** before jogging if you want more wallet events; decide in advance whether you are roleplaying **honor** or **greed** for the choice branch.
:::

## Special events

**Fishing** is often triggered as a **friend invite** question (not a single permanent “Fish” button): success can yield **raw fish**, **fishing skill**, and rare **money bag** luck. Random trackers can fire park-related prompts while you live your week.
