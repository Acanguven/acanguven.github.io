# Black market

The **black market** is in the **slum** district, not the polished downtown loop. Everything here is **crime-focused**: high rewards, **witness** risk, and possible **prison** fallout.

## What you can do

- **Smuggle goods** — Choose a **size** of run (small crate up to luxury). A **reaction mini-game**; you can **skip with gems** for a price tied to the payout. **Success**: large **cash**, **criminal** and **evil** skill gains; witnesses may still **add wanted days**. **Failure**: crime pressure **plus** you may face a **police chase** — lose that and you can land in prison. You can only smuggle again after a **monthly** cooldown.
- **Forge documents** — Pay **500**, confirm you accept the crime, then pass a **risk check** to get a **fake ID** (and a related achievement). Failure costs the attempt and adds **crime** pressure.

## When to visit

Only when your run can survive **25–40+ day** wanted spikes or a prison detour. A **burner phone** lowers witness odds when smuggling succeeds.

:::tip Strategy
If you are **already wanted**, the game skips the “are you sure?” step — faster flow, same risk.
:::

## Special events

**Witness** and **road escape** moments layer on top of smuggling. See **[Crime: Committing crimes](../crime/committing-crimes)** for how **heat** works.
