# Police station

The **police station** is where **lawful** and **wanted** stories collide: you can **turn yourself in** or **help the force** for pay and honor.

## What you can do

- **Surrender** — Visible while **wanted**. Cuts remaining sentence roughly in half (minimum one day) and sends you straight to **prison**. Use when heat is unbearable or you want to rip the bandage off.
- **Volunteer patrol** — Not wanted, not imprisoned. **Decision**-based night patrol: **energy** cost, **honor** + **communication** on success, cash stipend. Can spawn a **sub-decision** to confront a crook for bonus money or take a **health** hit if it goes wrong. Repeatable on a cooldown; many clears unlock **vigilante**-style achievements.

## When to visit

**Surrender** before random arrests screw a fragile run if you accept jail. **Patrol** is a mid-game **honor + money** grind when you are clean and can spare energy.

:::tip Strategy
Patrol’s optional **fight** branch is a skill check — raise **fighting** at the **[Gym](./gym)** if you hate losing health to failed interventions.
:::

## Special events

Crime module **pickpocket / GTA / smuggling** outcomes elsewhere can eventually **force** you here via wanted state; patrol is the main **benign** random chain at the building itself.
