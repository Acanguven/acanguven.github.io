# Social skills

Social skills shape **dialogue outcomes**, **job advancement**, **service careers**, and how you weather **stress**. They are often the difference between a promotion, a failed negotiation, or a calm exit from a bad event.

## Persuasion (max 300)

Used heavily in **social decisions** and **negotiations**—anything where you talk someone into (or out of) an outcome. Invest here if you like relationship-heavy play or want safer rolls on charm checks.

## Leadership (max 300)

**Leadership** feeds **job promotions** and **management** tracks. If you are climbing from line work into supervisory roles, keep this skill rising alongside job-specific skills.

## Communication (max 200)

Powers **social interactions** and customer-facing work such as **bartending** and **waitressing**. It is a workhorse for service careers and general likability checks.

## Honor (max 500)

The **moral** side of the axis: lawful, generous, or upright choices push **honor**. It is the **counter-skill to evil**—high honor runs feel like “clean” lives; it also gates some positive social outcomes.

## Evil (max 500)

**Crime-related** actions and ruthless decisions raise **evil**. It is the **counter to honor** and supports darker paths (theft, violence, intimidation). Expect tension between honor and evil on a single character.

## Calmness (max 200)

Helps in **stress situations**: high-pressure jobs, danger, or events that test nerves. Pair with decent **morale** recovery habits so you are not always one bad day from a spiral.

:::tip
Pick a **social identity** early: high **honor** + **persuasion** for “clean talker,” or **evil** + **calmness** for a cold criminal. Mixing extremes works but slows both axes compared to committing to one story.
:::

---

*Many social checks also look at relationships and context; skills raise your odds, not guaranteed wins.*
