# Skills overview

In **Life Simulator Reloaded**, **skills** are long-term proficiencies that gate jobs, fights, crimes, social checks, and module content (prison, celebrity, monster hunting). Each skill has a **maximum level**; you raise it by repeating relevant actions, jobs, education, and usable items.

## Learning speed and intelligence

Every point of **intelligence** adds **+0.002** to the progress you gain toward skills when you learn. Over a long life, a smart character levels professional and combat skills noticeably faster than a low-intelligence one—plan stats if you are aiming for a specialist build.

## Counter-pairs: honor and evil

**Honor** and **evil** are a **moral axis** pair: crime-heavy play pushes **evil**, while lawful or virtuous choices push **honor**. They cap each other narratively and mechanically as opposites—leaning into one path often means the other stays lower. Use this when deciding whether a run is a hero, antihero, or full outlaw.

:::tip
You do not need to max everything in one life. Pick **two or three pillars** (e.g. crime + prison life, or developer + computer) and let the rest rise passively from your job and story choices.
:::
