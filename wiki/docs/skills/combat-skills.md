# Combat skills

Combat-oriented skills decide how hard you hit in brawls, how athletic actions feel, and how effective ranged hunting gear is. All three are worth raising if you touch **crime**, **prison**, or the **Monster Hunter** module. None of these skills replace **health** management—carry heals for bad streaks.

## Fighting (max 500)

**Fighting** is the backbone of unarmed and melee-style confrontations: **street fights**, **prison brawls**, **prison attack** options, and the **monster punch** attack in hunts. It levels from winning engagements and from dedicated training loops (gym work, prison fight training, and similar repeated combat practice).

## Sports (max 200)

**Sports** supports general athletic performance. Train it at the **gym**, through sports-flavored **training** actions, and via moves like the **prison kick** attack. It also **combines with fighting** in some damage formulas—do not neglect it if you rely on punching your way out of problems.

## Aiming (max 200)

**Aiming** controls **rifle damage** in monster hunts (higher skill widens the damage range toward the top end) and improves consistency with ranged combat. You train it by **shooting the rifle** (chance per shot) and by **dagger**-related hunting practice. Pair it with **ammo stock** and a maintained rifle—see **[Hunting items](../items/hunting-items)**.

:::tip
If you only hunt occasionally, still fire the rifle when safe fights appear: **aiming** is slow to grow without live shots. For prison-heavy runs, **fighting + sports** matter more than aiming.
:::
