# Professional skills

These skills tie directly to **jobs**, **school**, **tech**, **crime**, **prison**, and **monster hunting**. Max levels differ by skill—check each row before you plan a grind.

| Skill | Max | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Programming** | 500 | **Developer** career progression and coding actions |
| **Economics** | 300 | **Banker** career and finance-flavored checks |
| **Mixology** | 200 | **Bartender** path |
| **Cooking** | 400 | **Cook** career |
| **Driving** | 300 | **Delivery** driver and vehicle-related work |
| **Acting** | 200 | **Actor** career (**Celebrity** module) |
| **Academics** | 300 | **School** performance and study outcomes |
| **Computer** | 200 | General **tech** actions and digital tasks |
| **Fishing** | 300 | Fishing activities and related rewards |
| **Cleaning** | 150 | Janitorial / service-style work |
| **Barber** | 150 | Hair and grooming services |
| **Crime** | 500 | **Crime success** rate and illegal job quality |
| **Survive in Streets** | 200 | **Beggar** survival and rough-living checks |
| **Prison Life** | 500 | **Prison** actions, routines, and **escape** difficulty |
| **Monster Lore** | 200 | **Monster detection** bonuses and synergies (e.g. **holy water** interactions) |

Training comes from the matching **job shifts**, **actions** at the right **locations**, **courses**, **items** (books, essences), and module-specific loops (kills, prison diary, etc.).

:::tip
For a **straight career run**, max the **one job skill** first, then **academics** or **communication** for stability. For **crime → prison** arcs, **crime** and **prison life** matter more than mixology—respec your time before sentencing.
:::

---

*Module content must be active in your save for skills like Acting or Monster Lore to shine.*
