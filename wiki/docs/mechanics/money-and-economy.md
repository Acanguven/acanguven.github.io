# Money & economy

Cash flow is the backbone of a stable life: rent, gear, education, and emergencies all pull from the same wallet. **Life Simulator Reloaded** ties income to **jobs**, stores wealth in **property**, and lets you trade through the **market**—while **gems** sit alongside as premium currency (see [Gems & boosts](./gems-and-boosts.md)).

## Jobs as income

- **Primary income:** Working shifts, promotions, and job-specific bonuses.
- **Skill gates:** Better jobs need skills; skills need time and sometimes **courses** (often a money sink upfront, payoff later).
- **Promotions:** **Looks** makes promotion skill checks a bit easier, so investing in appearance can help you climb pay tiers faster.

:::tip
Line up **one career track** early: bouncing between unrelated entry jobs burns days. Pick a path whose skills stack (e.g. service, tech, crime-adjacent) for compounding raises.
:::

## Property

Housing and related systems let you:

- Pay **rent** or invest in **better living** (comfort often feeds energy and morale indirectly through the actions available at home).
- Use **furniture** and home activities where the game offers them—some pieces give daily bonuses or extra options tied to being at home.

Property is both a **status symbol** and a **stability tool**: a safer home rhythm makes it easier to hold a job and recover your meters.

## Market: buying and selling

The **market** lists items with cash prices; some entries also offer a **gem** price. You can:

- **Buy** consumables, gear, gifts, and progression items.
- **Sell** surplus loot when the game lets you—clearing inventory turns clutter into runway.

Watch for **extra stock** unlocked by optional story packs: crime, celebrity, and monster content can add niche goods.

:::tip
Before big purchases, ask: *Does this unlock actions, save meters, or speed a quest?* Decorative buys are fine for flavor; progression buys win tight runs.
:::

## Assets panel

Once you have some savings, open the **Assets** panel to manage your wealth across three systems:

- **Housing** — Buy or rent properties, place furniture for stat bonuses, rent out extra properties for passive income. See: [Housing](../assets/housing.md).
- **Stock Market** — Invest in shares of 9 companies across 7 sectors. Prices change daily and react to news events — buy a **mobile phone** to receive alerts. See: [Stock Market](../assets/stock-market.md).
- **Collections** — Buy and sell collectibles at auction. Items appreciate over time. See: [Collections & Auctions](../assets/collections.md).

## Gem economy (overview)

Gems are **not** regular cash. They buy skips, boosts, and select market exclusives. Budget them for moments where failure costs **days** or **stats**—not for every small inconvenience. Details: [Gems & boosts](./gems-and-boosts.md).

## Earning money efficiently

1. **Promote on schedule** — meet skill checks before burning time on side hustles.
2. **Stack passive-friendly days** — work → recover → repeat beats constant risky grinds.
3. **Sell what you do not need** after events that flood inventory.
4. **Invest in education** when a course unlocks a higher wage tier; treat tuition as ROI, not sunk cost.
5. **Play the stock market** — buy stocks after bad news, sell on recovery. Even safe stocks like GoldBank beat leaving cash idle.
