# Gems & boosts

**Gems** are the premium currency in **Life Simulator Reloaded**. They are rarer than cash and are meant for **clutch saves**, **skips**, and **exclusive purchases**—not for bypassing every minor inconvenience.

## What gems buy

Typical spends include:

| Spend | Why use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Decision boosts** | Tip high-stakes choices in your favor (legal trouble, romance locks, career gambits). You can still fail, but the odds improve. |
| **Mini-game skips** | Instantly pass timed challenges when you are about to lose a reward or take a penalty. |
| **Market (gem-priced items)** | Gear, cosmetics, or utility items sold for gems instead of cash. |
| **Prison lawyer** | **15 gems** — hire representation for a large **sentence reduction** (a chunk of your remaining time, with a minimum cut); the game may limit how often this applies in a given situation. |
| **Guard bribe** | **5 gems** — some prison interactions expect a small gem payment (repeatable where the game allows). |

:::tip
Think in **days saved** and **unique rewards**: if a boost prevents a week of jail or saves a one-time reward, gems are well spent.
:::

## Decision boosts vs. mini-game skips

- **Boosts** improve your **chance of success** on a choice—you can still fail; use when the upside is huge and you are close to passing.
- **Skips** remove **having to play the challenge**—best when the mini-game is new to you or timing would ruin a fair attempt.

See [Mini-games](./mini-games.md) for per-game tactics so you need fewer skips over time.

## Best value purchases (general guidance)

1. **Lawyer (15 gems)** when you have **many prison days** left—a long sentence means a bigger slice of time saved.
2. **Skips** on **first-time** quest steps that gate **story packs** or **trophies** you cannot easily redo.
3. **Boosts** on **all-or-nothing** decisions where failure wipes **stats** or **relationships**.
4. **Gem market items** only for things that **unlock actions** or **lasting convenience**; skip pure flavor unless you enjoy the flex.

Avoid gem-spending loops on **problems you can solve with cash and time**—usually better to work another in-game week.

:::tip
Hoard a **small emergency stack** (e.g. 10–20 gems) if you play ironman-style; one bad encounter should not end a long run for lack of a few gems.
:::

## Earning gems

Income sources vary by how you play and by store offers (in-app purchases, rewards, events). Treat gems as **budgeted**, not **infinite**—the toughest spends assume you sometimes pay real money or accumulate slowly, so spend deliberately.

---

*Costs can change in updates—always check the in-game confirm dialog before you spend.*
