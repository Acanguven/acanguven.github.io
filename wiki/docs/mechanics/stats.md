# Stats guide

Your character’s numbers drive survival, careers, and special storylines. **Life Simulator Reloaded** separates *status meters* (what you feel today) from *core stats* (long-term potential) and *dynamic stats* (context-specific reputation).

## Status meters: health, energy, morale

| Meter | Range | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health** | 0 up to your maximum (you start at 100; some upgrades raise that cap) | Physical condition. |
| **Energy** | 0 up to your maximum | Stamina for actions and the day. |
| **Morale** | 0 up to your maximum | Mental well-being and motivation. |

- **Death:** If **any** of these three hits **0**, your character is considered dead—not only health.
- **Danger zone:** When **any** meter is **20 or below**, you are in danger (warnings and risky outcomes can follow). Stay above that line when you can.

Restore meters with rest, food, safe activities, and items or events that explicitly refill them. Pushing every day without recovery is how runs spiral.

:::tip
Treat **20** as your “red line” for health, energy, *and* morale. Heal and rest before you scrape the floor.
:::

## Core stats: intelligence & looks

| Stat | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intelligence** | 1–200 | Higher intelligence helps you **learn skills faster**, so jobs and training pay off sooner over a long career. |
| **Looks** | 1–200 | Higher looks **eases promotion checks**—employers judge you a bit more favorably on skill requirements, so social investment can open higher pay tiers. |

New characters start with both stats in a starter band; training, education, life choices, and items can move them over time. **Upgrades that raise your maximum** health, energy, or morale give you more room to work with during hard chapters (crime, fame grind, illness, and similar).

:::tip
High **intelligence** compounds over a long career; high **looks** softens promotion walls. Plan early if you want a specialist build vs. a social-climber build.
:::

## Dynamic stats

Some story paths add **dynamic stats** (they use the same kind of scale as your other stats while they matter), for example:

- **Prison reputation** — how inmates see you; affects intimidation, fights, and opportunities behind bars.
- **Fame** — celebrity visibility; amplifies outcomes in star-related content (films, events, public life).

These often rise from **actions tied to that storyline** (fights, performances, quests). They are not always visible from day one; they show up when the relevant content is in play.

## Quick reference: raising stats

| Goal | Typical levers |
| --- | --- |
| **Health / energy / morale** | Sleep, eat, low-risk routines, medical or wellness actions, story rewards. |
| **Intelligence** | School, reading, courses, brainy jobs and decisions. |
| **Looks** | Grooming, fitness, fashion, social and beauty-adjacent actions. |
| **Prison rep** | Dominance, winning fights, prison social actions (crime storyline). |
| **Fame** | Celebrity career path, media and public events (celebrity storyline). |
