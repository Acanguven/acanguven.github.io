# Prison quests (walkthrough)

All three quests **start automatically** when you **enter prison**. They can progress in parallel.

## Prison Survivor

**Goal:** Spend consecutive time locked up on the same sentence — milestones track **10, 20, and 30** days in prison (each day incarcerated counts).

| Milestone | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| First stretch | **10** days in prison |
| Second stretch | **20** days |
| Full run | **30** days |

**Reward:** **$100** when the quest completes.

**Survival tips**

- Keep **health**, **morale**, and **energy** out of the danger zone — at zero on key stats you can die.
- Use **yard**, **clinic**, food, and legal/social options to stabilize before picking risky fights.
- **Difficulty:** Medium if you are new to prison; easy once you learn the daily loop.

---

## Kingpin

**Milestones**

1. **Win three prison fights** (wins counted toward this quest).
2. **Form a gang** — choose **Form a Gang**: you need **strong Prison Reputation**, at least **two jail friends** with **friendship at least halfway** to the max, then you must **win** the initiation fight (success adds a few days to your sentence).
3. **Reach high standing in prison** — the quest talks about **80% prison rep**; in practice your **Prison Reputation needs to be very high** for this step to complete when days pass.

**Reward:** **$200**.

**Strategy**

- Raise **respect** safely: gym, jobs, gang meeting (after forming), some events — avoid losing streaks that tank rep.
- Befriend **inmates** with gifts/chat before attempting gang formation.
- **Difficulty:** Hard — rep grind + social setup + combat.

---

## Scholar

1. **Read five books** — prison **library** events and reading actions count toward this (not only the city library).
2. **Reach halfway mastery of the Prison Life skill** — you passively learn each prison day; books, counseling, and activities speed it up.

**Reward:** **$150**.

**Tips:** Visit library choices often; pair with low-risk days so you are not constantly in infirmary.

**Difficulty:** Medium.
