# Tutorial quests

Core tutorial quests teach **market**, **inventory**, **employment**, **housing**, **monster hunting**, and **friendships**.

## Staying Alive

**Goal:** Learn basic survival.

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Buy **water** from the **market**. |
| 2 | **Use** the water from inventory to recover health. |
| 3 | **Get a job** (any valid job for your age). |

**Reward:** Finishing this quest opens the way for **Quality Life** once you meet that quest’s requirements.

**Difficulty:** Easy — follow the on-screen milestones.

---

## The Hunter (monster hunter intro)

**Goal:** Hunting basics — gear up and score your **first zombie kill**.

1. Buy **zombie tracker** from the **Hunter's Guild**.
2. Buy **painkillers** at the **hospital**.
3. Buy **chainsaw** at the **gun shop**.
4. Buy **escape mirror** from the **Hunter's Guild**.
5. **Pass days** (sleep / advance time) until a zombie encounter resolves with a kill — the quest completes when that milestone is reached.

**Reward:** **$500**, **10 rifle ammo**, **5 painkillers**, **1 life stone**.

**Tips:** Entering the Hunter's Guild can start this quest if it is not active. Keep healing items for hunt events.

**Difficulty:** Easy–medium (depends on random hunt timing).

---

## Quality Life

**Goal:** Move from surviving to **stable independence**.

1. Get **promoted** at your job.
2. **Rent a house**.
3. Buy a **home decoration** item.
4. **Place** that item in your home.

**Reward:** Includes **$500** and meal items in-game.

**Difficulty:** Easy — requires income and housing flow.

---

## Social Butterfly

**Goal:** **Build meaningful friendships** (follow the in-game quest text — talk, hang out, and deepen relationships).

**Reward:** **$2000** on full completion.

**Difficulty:** Medium — needs social actions and time.
