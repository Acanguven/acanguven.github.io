# Adventure quests

Optional **lifestyle and exploration** arcs with varied milestones. Several begin in **young adulthood** (ages **18–23**).

## Childhood Adventures

**Starts:** Around **age 4**. **Must complete before age 10.**

**Milestones (4):**

1. Play on the **playground**.
2. Take a **nap**.
3. Play with **toys**.
4. **Meditate**.

**Reward:** **$50** and a **teddy bear**. Completing the quest also unlocks the **Childhood Champion** achievement.

**Difficulty:** Easy — plan a few child-years ahead so you do not age out.

---

## Social Explorer

**Four milestones** — mix of **selfie**, **arm wrestle**, **deep conversation**, and **sports** with other people (see in-game text).

**Difficulty:** Medium — needs NPCs or friends available for each interaction type.

---

## Brave Adventurer

**Five milestones:** haunted house, **marathon**, escape room, **cave**, **bungee jump**.

**Difficulty:** Medium–hard (some activities are rare or stat-gated).

---

## Moral Compass

**Five milestones:** ethical choices such as **rejecting a bribe**, helping an **accident victim**, refusing **stolen goods**, **reporting cheating**, **returning a found phone**.

**Difficulty:** Medium — watch for decision popups in the world.

---

## Fitness Journey

**Four milestones:** sports sessions, **marathon**, **gym with someone** (5×), **blood donation**.

**Difficulty:** Medium.

---

## Knowledge Seeker

**Four milestones:** finish **3 courses**, **study together** 10×, **teach skills** 5×, join a **book club**.

**Difficulty:** Medium — pairs well with education and social systems.

---

## Community Hero

**Five milestones:** shelter **volunteering**, **petition**, **blood donation**, help a **lost tourist**, **rescue an animal**.

**Difficulty:** Medium.

---

## Wedding Bells

**Four milestones:**

1. Buy **wedding outfit**.
2. **Plan** the wedding.
3. **Invite** someone to the wedding.
4. **Get married**.

**Difficulty:** Medium — needs a relationship path advanced enough to marry.
