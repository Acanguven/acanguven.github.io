# Monster hunter quests

Monster content lives in the **Monster Hunter** module. You need the **zombie tracker** and guild access early; late game centers on the **Dark Forest** and **tracker upgrades**.

## The Hunter (see Tutorial Quests)

Full step-by-step and rewards (**$500**, **10 rifle ammo**, **5 painkillers**, **1 life stone**) are on **[Tutorial Quests](./tutorial-quests.md)**.

**Difficulty:** Easy–medium.

## Master of the Hunt

**Prerequisite:** **Zombie tracker level 3** — you cannot enter the **Dark Forest** until your tracker is at least **level 3**. Upgrade the tracker at the Hunter's Guild as you earn kills and currency.

### Milestones (recommended order)

1. **Visit the Dark Forest** — your first visit also earns the **Into the Darkness** achievement.
2. **Kill a skeleton** — mid-tier hunts; tracker level 3+ helps spawns.
3. **Kill a witch** — same tier band as forest content.
4. **Kill a demon** — hardest common type; prepare healing and strong weapons.
5. **Reach tracker level 5** — guild upgrades; max tier unlocks top detection.

**Reward:** **$2000**, **5 demon horns**, **10 holy water**.

### Step-by-step guide

1. Finish **The Hunter** tutorial quest so you understand hunt pacing and the **escape mirror**.
2. Farm **zombies** and lower hunts until you can afford **tracker level 3**.
3. Enter **Dark Forest**; prioritize **skeleton → witch → demon** as encounters appear (results vary by chance).
4. After kills register, bank money and materials for **levels 4–5** upgrades.
5. Watch the **quest log** for milestone updates after each kill and after the final upgrade.

**Tips:** Painkillers and good weapons reduce failed hunts. Use the mirror when a run goes badly.

**Difficulty:** Hard — long grind + tough fights + economy for upgrades.

## Related achievements

While hunting you will often unlock milestones such as **Hunter Candidate**, **Amateur Hunter**, **Professional Hunter**, **Legendary Hunter**, **Bone Crusher**, **Witch Hunter**, **Demon Fighter**, **Monster Encyclopedia**, **Mass Extinction**, **Into the Darkness**, and **Getting Serious** (max tracker). See **[Monster Hunter Achievements](../achievements/monster-hunter-achievements.md)** for a full list and how to earn them.
