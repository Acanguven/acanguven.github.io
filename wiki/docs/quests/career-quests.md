# Career quests

These quests reward **grinding a job or school path** and mastering promotions.

## Beggar Career

**Starts:** Automatically when you take the **beggar** job.

| Milestone | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Street grind | **Beg 100 times** (tracked on your character). |
| Status | Promote to **Owner of the region** (top beggar rank). |
| Income | Earn **$500 total** from begging (tracked separately from the 100 begs). |

**Reward:** **$150** in current game data (some older notes may say **$100**).

**Tips:** Work outside **home** so beg actions and day-pass ticks count. Train **persuasion** and **survive in streets** for promotions.

**Difficulty:** Medium (time sink).

---

## Bartender Career

1. Serve **100 cocktails** on the job.
2. Reach **Master** bartender rank.
3. Earn **$300** in **tips** total.

**Tips:** Stack shifts at the bar; higher skill and good decisions improve tips.

**Difficulty:** Medium.

---

## Waitress Career

1. Serve **50 tables**.
2. Promote to **Senior** waitress.
3. Earn **$200** in **tips**.

**Tips:** Similar to bartender — consistent work and successful service events.

**Difficulty:** Medium.

---

## School Life

1. **Pass** a school exam.
2. **Perform** in a talent show or debate.
3. **Help a teacher** (school interaction milestone).

**Difficulty:** Easy–medium — stay enrolled and attend school events.

---

## Teen hustle

**Starts:** Around **age 14**.

1. Land your **first part-time job**.
2. Get **promoted** at that job.

**Tips:** Check job board / available teen jobs early so the quest has time to finish.

**Difficulty:** Easy.
