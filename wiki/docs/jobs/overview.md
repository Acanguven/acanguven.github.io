# Jobs & careers overview

In **Life Simulator Reloaded**, work is how you earn steady money, train skills, and unlock quests. Most jobs require you to be **not imprisoned**, pass **age gates**, and meet **skill or course** thresholds as you climb ranks.

## How hiring works

When a job is available, you apply and may face an **interview**: scripted questions where your answers and stats can affect the outcome. Some roles (for example teen part-time work) reuse a compact set of **teen interview** prompts; specialized careers use longer, role-specific question pools.

## Promotions

Each job defines **levels** (titles) with **payment**, **skills learned per day**, and **requirements** (skills, stats, or completed courses). A **promotion ratio** rolls over time—keep working, stay qualified, and you can move up. Higher ranks usually pay more but demand stronger skills.

:::tip
Before chasing a promotion, open the job’s level list and note **hard gates** (for example a **course requirement** or a skill floor). Finish the course or grind the skill *before* you stall for many in-game days.
:::

## Interview tips

- Prefer answers that match the **job theme** (safety for acrobat, responsibility for teen jobs, composure for service roles).
- **Communication**, **calmness**, and **sports** help across many interviews; invest early if you job-hop often.
- If you fail an interview, improve skills and try again—there is no shame in treating it like a skill check.
