# Waitress

**Waitresses** run the **restaurant floor**: **communication**-heavy shifts, **table service** statistics, and a **tip tracker** separate from base wages. The job pairs naturally with **customer service** training and social stat growth.

## Key skills

- **Communication** — primary requirement for promotions and many random events.
- **Cleaning** shows up at trainee levels because spills and resets are part of the shift.

:::tip
Grab the **customer service** course while you are still a **trainee**. It is tailored to this ladder and softens both promotions and angry-customer events.
:::

## Shift gameplay

Expect **questions** about allergies, mixed-up orders, rush hour, and manager feedback. **Decisions** sometimes pit etiquette against speed—higher communication makes the “smooth” branches reliable.

## Career quest: Waitress Career

Milestones usually include:

1. **Serve 50 tables** — accumulates as you work the floor.
2. **Promote to senior waitress** — proves you cleared the mid-rank skill checks.
3. **Earn 200 in tips** — tracked across your waitress career, not a single shift.

Cash plus bragging rights reward players who commit to service.

## Strategy

- Keep **morale** healthy; rude customers are scripted to drain it.
- If you chase the quest, **prioritize tip events** when your communication can carry the skill check.
- Transition to **bartender** or **cook** later if you want the same location with different skills.

:::tip
**Looks** helps many social careers indirectly by easing requirement math—if promotions stall despite high communication, check whether stat gates are hiding on the next level.
:::
