# Bartender

**Bartenders** work the **bar** location: high **mixology** demand, steady **communication** growth, and a strong **tip economy** driven by random shift events. It is one of the clearest “service plus craft” loops in the game.

## Key skills

- **Mixology** — primary craft stat; gates every promotion after the entry level.
- **Communication** — customer handling, drunk patrons, and many event branches.
- **Calmness** can appear when you de-escalate instead of brawling.

:::tip
The **mixology course** is not optional for the mid ladder: **master bartender** expects that training. Buy and finish it before you camp the bar expecting a promotion.
:::

## Day loop

While employed, the game tends to **move you to the bar** for workdays. Shifts increment **cocktails served** (with variance), train mixology, and sometimes communication. Random events fire often—tips, recipe quizzes, trouble customers, and occasional fights.

## Career quest: Bartender Career

Typical milestones include:

1. **Serve 100 cocktails** — comes naturally from working; tracks with service statistics.
2. **Promote to master bartender** — proves you cleared the rank and course gates.
3. **Earn 300 in tips** — cumulative tips from bar life (event-driven).

Reward money helps you pivot to another career or fund education.

:::tip
Tips are **event-driven**. If the quest stalls, keep working and favor **tip-friendly** event choices when skills allow—do not only wait for base wages.
:::

## Strategy

Stack **communication** early so drunk-customer and rush events hurt less. Keep **health and energy** out of the danger zone; bar fights and bad shifts punish low pools hard.
