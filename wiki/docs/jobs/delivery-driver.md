# Delivery driver

**Delivery drivers** move goods (and sometimes stranger cargo) for **driving-skill** pay. It is a practical career for players who want **fast employment**, **vehicle fantasy**, and a bridge into **secure commercial** driving without a college degree.

## Key skills

- **Driving** — the entire ladder; promotions ask for ever-higher mastery.
- Physical stats indirectly help chase events, but the job checks **driving** first.

:::tip
Enroll in **driving license** training the moment you hit minimum age. Without it, you are guessing at skill growth instead of compounding **daily course gains** plus work gains.
:::

## Education

Two specialist-style courses stack:

1. **Driving license** — foundational, shorter, cheaper.
2. **Secure driving** — advanced, longer, pricier; **requires** the license course.

Finish both before you expect **elite delivery** titles or weapon-bank style routes that assume professional competence.

## Gameplay loop

Shifts combine **driving checks**, **time pressure**, and occasional **decisions** about shortcuts or suspicious packages. Riskier branches pay better when you succeed; crashes or tickets hurt **money** and **morale**.

## Strategy

- Keep **energy** high—long routes punish tired characters.
- If you use delivery only as a **funding job**, stop after license + one promotion tier and pivot tuition to **developer** or **banker**.
- Pair with **mechanic** fantasies only if you enjoy repair side content in other systems.

:::tip
Delivery is the best **“pay for school”** job: strong wages relative to education time, minimal social interview pressure compared to bartender or actress tracks.
:::
