# Acrobat

The **acrobat** career is **performance plus risk**: juggler openings, balance acts, fire dancing, and **master acrobat** prestige. **Sports** is the backbone stat, and the job’s **interview** questions already warn you that injuries are part of the gig.

## Key skills

- **Sports** — trains every day and gates each promotion tier.
- Some events still test **morale** and **health** because tricks fail loudly.

:::tip
Before you sign up, bank **extra health and energy**—failed stunt decisions can chunk your bars. The career is unforgiving if you live permanently in the “danger zone” thresholds.
:::

## Dynamic decisions

Acrobat work opens **acrobatic movement** decisions: choose flashy routines, safe rehearsals, or crowd-pleasing risks. Success spikes pay and fame-style feelings; failure sends you toward **hospital** visits or lost wages.

## Interviews

Expect questions about **injury**, **teamwork**, and **motivation**. The game treats acrobat hiring like casting for a circus: confidence matters, but reckless answers can backfire in subtle ways.

## Strategy

1. Start only after **teen sports jobs** or gym habits raised **sports** ~30+.
2. Alternate risky shows with **rest days** so random injuries do not chain.
3. Exit gracefully into **actor** if you want performance without physical pain.

:::tip
If you chase achievements tied to **stunt survival**, save **gems** for boosts on the hardest acrobat decisions—those checks are classic gem sinks.
:::
