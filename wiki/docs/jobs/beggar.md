# Beggar

The **beggar** path is a full **street-life career**: you earn outside conventional employers, train **persuasion** and **survive in streets**, and face a constant stream of **random events** and **decisions**—including the infamous **pickpocket** branch with high risk and high drama.

## Core skills

- **Survive in streets** — resilience to weather, hunger, and trouble; rises from daily work and street events.
- **Persuasion** — better outcomes when begging, negotiating, or talking your way out of problems.
- **Fighting** and **sports** show up in chase and conflict options tied to street events.

:::tip
Treat **survive in streets** as your defensive stat and **persuasion** as your income engine. If both lag, you will fail skill checks on **decisions** long before you starve.
:::

## Career quest: Beggar Career

When you take the job, the **Beggar Career** quest can track three milestones:

1. **Beg 100 times** — grind the beg action and daily flow until the counter hits the target.
2. **Become district leader** — promote through job levels until you hold **district leader** rank.
3. **Earn 500 total from begging** — cumulative income from begging-related earnings (tracked for the quest).

Completing the arc pays a **lump cash reward** and marks you as someone who mastered the district.

## Unique decisions and risk

Beggar life is decision-heavy: turf disputes, police patrols, gang pressure, and **pickpocket** opportunities. Success can spike **morale** and **money**; failure can drain **health**, **morale**, and **cash**. Read skill requirements before you tap the risky option.

:::tip
If you are saving for a clean exit from the street, **avoid high-variance decisions** until you have a cushion. Pickpocket is a story moment—not a stable salary.
:::

## Education synergy

Street-focused **courses** (street survival workshop, persuasion training) accelerate the same skills interviews and promotions expect. They are cheap compared to college tracks and pair well with this career.
