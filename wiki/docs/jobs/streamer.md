# Streamer

The **streamer** job is part of the **celebrity** module. You build a personal **channel**, grow **subscribers**, earn money from **live streams**, and climb through six career tiers as your **fame** rises. Unlike acting, streaming is driven by **your equipment** and **daily engagement** rather than audition luck.

## Requirements

You need a **laptop** or **gaming PC** to become a streamer. Without one, the job appears locked with a message telling you to buy a computer first. Head to the **electronics shop** to pick one up.

:::tip
A **gaming PC** ($2,500) is much better than a laptop for streaming — it gives a streaming performance boost, higher viewer counts, and better earnings. You can upgrade it later for even more bonuses.
:::

## Channel setup

When you first join the streamer job, a **prompt** asks you to personalize your channel:

- **Channel name** — pick anything up to 20 characters (or get a random one)
- **Content category** — gaming, IRL, music, tech, or creative

Your choice of category affects which **stream topics** appear during your career. You can see your channel info on the **employment screen**.

## Key skills and stats

- **Streaming** — primary skill; trained on the job and through the **streaming basics** course
- **Communication** — grows from chat interactions and live sessions
- **Fame** — the dynamic stat that tracks your public profile; gates promotions at higher ranks
- **Basic computer** — entry requirement and grows from analytics work

## Career levels

| Level | Title | Key requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Newbie | Basic computer skill |
| 2 | Affiliate | Streaming + communication |
| 3 | Partner | High streaming + streaming basics course |
| 4 | Popular | Streaming + fame 30 + communication |
| 5 | Star | Streaming + fame 80 + persuasion |
| 6 | Legend | Fame 150 + streaming + leadership |

## Education

Complete the **Streaming Basics** course (apprenticeship tier) — it is required to reach the **partner** level and above. The course is affordable and teaches foundational streaming knowledge.

## Streams and subscribers

Your career revolves around two loops:

### Passive streams (day-pass events)

While employed, the game periodically offers you a **stream series** — a multi-day run with a specific topic, estimated viewers, and projected earnings. Accept to go live; the stream runs for several days with random events (trolls, raids, donations, copyright strikes, tech failures, viral moments) and ends with a payout based on **fame**, **streaming skill**, **subscriber count**, and **PC level**.

### Active actions

Three privileged actions appear at the top of your action list while streaming:

- **Go Live** — an impromptu **tap targets** mini-game. Success earns money (scales with subscribers), fame, and new subscribers. Cooldown: 3 days.
- **Interact with Chat** — available during an active stream series. Boosts viewer count and trains communication and persuasion. Cooldown: 5 days.
- **Review Analytics** — requires a PC. Trains streaming and computer skills. Cooldown: 10 days.

:::tip
**Go Live** earnings scale with your subscriber count — the more followers you have, the more each live session pays. At 500 subscribers you earn roughly $100 extra per session.
:::

## Gaming PC

The **gaming PC** is a key item for serious streamers. Buy it at the **electronics shop** for $2,500, then upgrade it up to **level 5** for increasing bonuses.

### Upgrade path

Upgrades are done at the **electronics shop** via the **Upgrade Gaming PC** action. Each level costs money:

| Level | Cost | Cumulative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | $500 | $500 |
| 2 → 3 | $1,000 | $1,500 |
| 3 → 4 | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| 4 → 5 | $2,000 | $5,000 |

### What upgrades do

Each PC level improves your streaming in multiple ways:

- **+3 base viewers** per level to every stream
- **10% earnings multiplier** per level (up to 50% at max)
- **Better mini-game** — targets last longer and spawn faster, making Go Live easier
- **More subscriber gains** at stream end

:::info
Maxing out your gaming PC to level 5 unlocks the **Ultimate Setup** achievement.
:::

## Random events

While a stream series is running, you will encounter events that test your decisions:

- **Troll in chat** — handle toxic viewers
- **Tech failure** — your setup crashes mid-stream
- **Viral clip** — a moment blows up online
- **Donation message** — a big donor with a weird request
- **Stream raid** — another streamer sends their audience to you
- **Copyright strike** — music or content gets flagged
- **IRL recognized** — someone recognizes you from your streams
- **Burnout warning** — the grind catches up to you

You will also face **decisions** like sponsor deals, stream controversies, and collaboration invites. These affect your fame, money, and subscriber count.

## Strategy

1. **Buy a gaming PC first** — a laptop works but the PC gives meaningful bonuses from day one.
2. **Complete streaming basics early** — it gates the partner level.
3. **Upgrade your PC when you can afford it** — each level pays for itself quickly through better earnings.
4. **Accept stream series** — passive income and subscriber growth happen during active streams.
5. **Use Go Live on cooldown** — the mini-game is the fastest way to earn money and subscribers manually.
6. **Watch your energy** — streaming actions cost energy; keep it above danger zone to avoid missing streams.

:::warning
If you resign from the streamer job, your active stream series is abandoned. Your channel, subscribers, and PC upgrades are preserved if you return to streaming later.
:::
