# Banker

**Bankers** sit at the **bank** and climb a long **finance ladder**: **money sorter** and **cashier** early, **mortgage** and **management** mid-game, and **executive** heights for patient players. The defining skill is **economics**, backed by formal **education**.

## Key skills

- **Economics** — required at every meaningful promotion; grows from courses and job experience.
- Soft skills like **communication** help indirectly in customer-facing bank events, but the hard gates are economic.

:::tip
Complete **economics apprenticeship** before you dream of **college economics**. The college program **requires** the apprenticeship certificate—same pattern as developer, different skill.
:::

## Education path

1. **Apprenticeship economics** — moderate price, ~two in-game months, caps skill at a trainee-friendly ceiling.
2. **College economics** — higher price and duration, raises the ceiling toward manager roles.

Bank pay scales slowly at first; you are paying for **late-game title jumps**, not day-one riches.

## Work fantasy

Expect paperwork, client advice, and occasional **ethical decisions** about loans or VIP clients. Good choices lean on **economics** checks; bad ones hit **morale** or **reputation** style outcomes.

## Strategy

- Fund courses with **delivery** or **school jobs** if you start from zero cash.
- Raise **intelligence** for faster skill absorption if you plan multiple finance degrees later.
- Stack **financial literacy** (persuasion-adjacent) only if you want cross-career negotiation perks.

:::tip
Banker pairs well with **developer** lives only sequentially—both demand long schooling. Pick **one** white-collar fantasy per character unless you enjoy slow-burn saves.
:::
