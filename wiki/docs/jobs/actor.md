# Actor

The **actor** job lives in the **celebrity** module: **acting** skill progression, **looks** and **intelligence** stat gates, and a visible **fame** dynamic stat that tracks how known you are. It is the cleanest “become famous” career if you commit to film logic.

## Key skills and stats

- **Acting** — trained on the job and through **acting courses**.
- **Looks** gates many ranks (camera-ready requirements).
- **Intelligence** matters for voice work and early tiers.

:::tip
Finish **acting starter** before you expect **background** or **supporting** roles. Several levels hard-require the starter course via **course requirements**—talent alone is not enough.
:::

## Fame

Fame rises from employment hooks and celebrity events. Higher fame unlocks **better pay**, stranger **random encounters**, and more **decision pressure** (paparazzi, scandals, luxury problems).

## Education

1. **Apprenticeship acting** — affordable entry, raises skill ceiling for junior roles.
2. **Professional acting (college tier)** — expensive, requires starter completion, pushes you toward lead-tier fantasy.

## Strategy

- Alternate **work** with **morale care**; rejection events exist even when you are famous.
- Invest in **public speaking** or **communication** courses if interviews feel harder than performances.
- Plan money for **styling** items or looks boosts if promotions stall on stat checks.

:::tip
Actor is module-gated—enable **celebrity** content in your run before you roadmap this path, or you will not see the job at all.
:::
