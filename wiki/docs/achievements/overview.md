# Achievements overview

**Achievements** in **Life Simulator Reloaded** are permanent badges for hitting milestones across **life sim**, **crime**, **celebrity**, and **monster hunter** content. They are organized in the app by **module** and theme (social, combat, career, and so on).

## How unlocking works

- Most achievements unlock when you complete a **specific action** successfully (win a fight, buy a lawyer, release a movie at a certain tier, and similar milestones).
- Some badges depend on **running totals**—days alive, fights won, books read, and so on. When you cross the required number, the achievement unlocks **once**.
- Some achievements are **mutually exclusive** with failure outcomes (for example succeeding at a riot versus failing it). Read prompts carefully so you know which path you are on.

## Organization by module

| Module | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Core** | Long life, cooking, romance, weddings, quizzes, boxing, gardening |
| **Crime** | Pickpocket, car theft, prison life, escapes, gang, lawyer |
| **Celebrity** | Fame thresholds, movie counts and rarities |
| **Monster Hunter** | First kills per type, 500 kills, top tracker level, Dark Forest |

## Tips for achievement hunting

1. **Play one theme per life** — prison, acting, or hunting each need **time**; mixing goals spreads your attention thin.
2. **Use the diary / log** — pop-ups often confirm progress toward goals that are not spelled out on screen.
3. **Gems** — a few crime options (for example **hiring a lawyer**) cost gems on purpose; save currency if you are collecting those badges.
4. **Difficulty labels** (used in subpages): **Easy** = one or two sessions; **Medium** = needs planning; **Hard** = long grind or strict requirements.

## If an achievement will not unlock

- Confirm the **module** is enabled and you are not in a **blocking** situation (for example you are dead, or you are outside prison when trying to earn a prison-only badge).
- Some badges only count **success** — failing a mini-game may not move progress forward.
- **Day-based** checks often update when a new day starts; sleep or advance time after you think you have met the last requirement.
- Game updates can change numbers or triggers slightly; if something feels off after a patch, check the in-game achievement list for the latest wording.
