# Celebrity achievements

**Celebrity** content centers on **fame** and **film releases**. Achievements expect you to pursue the **actor** career path (or other sources of fame the game offers).

## Fame thresholds

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Famous** | Reach **100 fame** | Take movie roles, PR events, and fame-positive actions | Medium |
| **Hall of Fame** | Reach **200 fame** | Double down on high-tier roles and publicity | Hard |

**Strategy:** Fame rises slowly—avoid long prison stretches or scandals that wipe fame unless you are role-playing a comeback.

## Movie count

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **First Movie** | **Release** your **first** movie | Finish a production cycle as an actor | Easy |
| **Movie Star** | Release **10 different** movies | Vary projects; stay employed long term | Hard |

Progress counts **distinct** releases—repeating the same project may not advance this if the game treats each title separately; follow career prompts to see what counts.

## Movie rarity tiers

Each requires you to **release** a movie of that **rarity** (better scripts, budgets, and outcomes skew toward higher tiers):

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Uncommon Movie** | Release an **uncommon** movie | Better scripts, skills, or budgets | Medium |
| **Rare Movie** | Release a **rare** movie | Higher requirements than uncommon | Medium–hard |
| **Epic Movie** | Release an **epic** movie | Late-career gear and stats | Hard |
| **Legendary Movie** | Release a **legendary** movie | Top-tier luck and investment | Hard |

## Hunting path (one life)

1. Rush **First Movie** early for the easy badge.
2. Stack **fame** actions until **100**, then push **200** for **Hall of Fame**.
3. Grind **10 movies** while chasing higher rarities—**epic** and **legendary** show up more often with stronger **skills** and **better roles**.

**Difficulty summary:** Fame badges are **medium** time sinks; the full movie ladder is **hard** because of rarity luck and how long a career needs to run.

## Streaming achievements

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **First Stream** | Complete your first streaming series | Accept a stream series offer as a streamer | Easy |
| **100 Subscribers** | Reach **100** subscribers | Keep streaming and use Go Live regularly | Easy |
| **1000 Subscribers** | Reach **1,000** subscribers | Long-term streaming career with consistent engagement | Medium |
| **Gone Viral** | Have one of your clips go viral | Triggered by random viral clip events during streams | Medium |
| **Sponsored** | Complete your first sponsorship deal | Accept a sponsor deal decision during a stream | Easy |
| **Ultimate Setup** | Upgrade your gaming PC to **level 5** | Costs $5,000 total in upgrades at the electronics shop | Medium |

### Streaming path (one life)

1. Start streaming and accept your first series for **First Stream**.
2. Use **Go Live** on cooldown and accept stream offers to push toward **100** then **1,000 subscribers**.
3. Watch for **sponsor deal** decisions during active streams for an easy **Sponsored** badge.
4. Upgrade your gaming PC whenever you can afford it — **Ultimate Setup** at level 5.
5. **Gone Viral** is event-driven; keep streaming and it will eventually trigger.
