# Crime & prison achievements

## Prison — quick reference

| Achievement | How to unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Graffiti** | Use **spray paint** on a prison wall | Prison item or action when available | Easy |
| **Very Regretful** | Use the **cry** action in prison | Lowers morale; exists for this moment | Easy |
| **Show Some Respect!** | **Win** any **prison fight** | Train combat; pick winnable targets | Medium |
| **You Can't Chase Me** | **Succeed** at escaping during a **wanted** / police chase | Home or travel chase decisions | Medium |
| **Jailbreaker** | **Succeed** at the **prison escape** mini-game | High risk; keep health up | Hard |
| **Riot Starter** | **Succeed** at starting a **riot** | Needs **prison reputation at 100 or higher** and **Prison Life skill at 30% or higher** | Hard |
| **Gang Leader** | **Form a gang** | **Reputation 80+**, **at least two jail friends** at **50+** friendship, and win the initiation fight | Hard |
| **Survivor** | **30** days incarcerated (counts over multiple sentences) | Stay inside; survive the daily loop | Medium |
| **Veteran Inmate** | **100** days incarcerated | Long sentence or repeat visits | Hard |
| **Smuggler** | **Succeed** at a **smuggle** mini-game or event | Yard and contraband opportunities | Medium |
| **Prison Scholar** | **Read 5 or more books** in the **prison library** | Choose read options in library events | Medium |
| **Brawler** | Win **5** prison fights | Repeat yard and combat events | Medium |
| **Fight Club** | Win **10** prison fights | Higher exposure to injury | Hard |
| **Prison Champion** | **Defeat the prison champion** | Random event after **5+** fight wins; not guaranteed immediately | Hard |
| **Craftsman** | **Craft a shiv** in the workshop | Gather materials and use the workshop action | Medium |
| **Tattooed** | Use the **tattoo kit** | Prison item use | Easy |
| **Lawyered Up** | **Hire a lawyer** | Costs **15 gems**; sentence must be **more than 15** days left | Medium (currency) |
| **Early Release** | **Succeed** at a **parole** hearing | Good behavior and the parole event chain | Hard |
| **Took On The Law** | **Win** a fight against a **guard** | Random guard encounter | Hard |
| **Guardian** | **Defend a friend** in a prison interaction | Needs a friend in jail and the right event | Medium |

### Prison strategy notes

- **Prison reputation** gates gang formation and riots; it also **drops** over time if you do not keep it up—plan social and combat loops so it does not decay away.
- **Champion** and **guard** fights show up from **events**; stay healthy and keep winning fights to make them appear.
- For **Prison Scholar**, prioritize library prompts on **safe** days.

## Street crime (non-prison)

| Achievement | How to unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sticky Fingers** | **First successful pickpocket** | Often through beggar or street decisions; skill checks apply | Medium |
| **Grand Theft Auto** | **First successful car theft** | Crime actions once unlocked | Medium |
| **Smooth Criminal** | **Smuggle successfully 3 times** | Contraband and smuggle loops | Medium |
| **Master Forger** | **Successfully forge** identity documents | Forgery job or crime path | Hard |

Mixing **wanted** status with wedding or job milestones can derail plans—time crime-heavy runs when you are focused on those badges.
