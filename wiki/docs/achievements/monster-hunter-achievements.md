# Monster hunter achievements

These badges track **hunts**, **tracker upgrades**, and **Dark Forest** exploration. You need the **Monster Hunter** module enabled.

## First kills (per type)

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hunter Candidate** | **First zombie** kill | Tutorial quest line; sleep until hunts appear early | Easy |
| **Bone Crusher** | **First skeleton** | Usually after **tracker level 3** and deeper forest access | Medium |
| **Witch Hunter** | **First witch** | Dark Forest and mid-tier spawns | Medium |
| **Demon Fighter** | **First demon** | Toughest routine type; bring heals and strong weapons | Hard |

## Encyclopedia & grind

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Monster Encyclopedia** | Kill **at least one** of **each** monster **type** | Zombie, skeleton, witch, and demon | Hard |
| **Mass Extinction** | **500** total kills (all types combined) | Long career; mix easier zombies with bigger game | Hard |

You can work toward **500** kills while pursuing **Master of the Hunt**—both reward staying active as a hunter.

## Zombie rarity

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |

| **The Warrior** | Kill an **epic** zombie | Higher tracker level and random encounters | Medium–hard |
| **Dead Eye** | Kill a **legendary** zombie | Rarest spawns; endgame gear helps | Hard |

## Demon & boss flair

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Demon Slayer** | Kill a **legendary demon** | Top-tier demon fight outcome | Hard |

## Items & locations

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Are you scared?** | Use the **escape mirror** during a hunt | Buy from the Hunter's Guild; keep it in inventory | Easy |
| **Getting Serious** | Upgrade the **zombie tracker** to **level 5** | Costs kills and money; guild upgrades | Hard |
| **Into the Darkness** | **First visit** to the **Dark Forest** | Needs **tracker level 3** to enter | Medium |

## Route suggestion

1. Finish **The Hunter** (second tutorial quest) for basics and gear.
2. Upgrade to **tracker level 3**, then enter the **Dark Forest** (**Into the Darkness**).
3. Clear **one of each monster type** for **Monster Encyclopedia** while pushing the tracker to **level 5**.
4. Farm remaining kills toward **500** and chase **legendary** encounters last, when your gear and stats are strongest.

**Overall difficulty:** **Medium–hard** — random encounters and economy gate the late tiers.
