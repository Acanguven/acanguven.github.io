# Core achievements

Highlights from the **core** module—milestones most long lives will touch at least once.

## Longevity

| Achievement | Unlock (summary) | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **100 Days** | Survive **100** days | Keep happiness, energy, and morale above zero | Easy |
| **500 Days** | Survive **500** days | Stable job, housing, and health habits | Medium |
| **1000 Days** | Survive **1000** days | Long-run character; avoid crime spirals that cut lives short | Hard |
| **2000 Days** | Survive **2000** days | Dedicated marathon save | Hard |

## Childhood

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Childhood Champion** | Finish the **Childhood Adventures** quest before age **10** | Start around age 4; use playground, nap, toys, and meditate actions | Easy |

## Food & home

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Junior Chef** | **Cook** for the first time | Kitchen or cook job actions | Easy |
| **Green Thumb** | **Harvest herbs** from your garden **3** times | Maintain garden plots | Medium |

## Social & romance

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hookup** | **Hook up** with someone | Dating and night-life interactions | Easy |
| **Serious Step For Love** | **Get engaged** | Deepen the relationship and follow the proposal flow | Medium |
| **Happily Ever After** | **Get married** | Follow marriage steps (overlaps with the **Wedding Bells** quest line) | Medium |
| **Wedding Planner** | **Plan a perfect wedding** | Wedding prep choices and a high-quality outcome | Medium |
| **Honeymoon Bliss** | **Go on a honeymoon** | Post-wedding travel event | Medium |

## Activities & combat

| Achievement | Unlock | Tips | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fishing Money** | Find a **money bag** while fishing | Fish repeatedly; luck involved | Medium |
| **Quiz Master** | Win **5** pub **quiz** nights | Bar quizzes on their schedule | Medium |
| **Knockout King** | Win **5** **boxing** matches | Gym or boxing path; train fitness | Medium |

## Hunting strategy

- Stack **compatible** goals: marriage line + **Wedding Planner** + **Honeymoon Bliss** in one adult arc.
- For **Quiz Master**, watch **night** availability and your energy.
- **Knockout King** needs you in fighting shape—do not enter matches with no health left.

Many more core badges exist (travel, debates, karaoke, mentoring, and others)—explore locations and social actions to discover them naturally.
