# Housing

Housing is one of the most important investments in the game. Your home determines your **maximum stats**, gives **daily stat bonuses** from both the property itself and any furniture you place, and can even generate **passive income** when you rent out properties you own.

Open the **Assets** panel, then tap **House** to access two tabs: **Properties** and **Furniture**.

## Properties

The Properties tab shows your current home at the top, followed by all available properties. Each property card displays its **value** (buy price), **rent price** (daily cost if renting), **capacity** (furniture slots), **maintenance cost** (daily cost if owned), and **stat effects**.

### Renting vs. buying

You have two ways to move in:

- **Rent** — Pay the daily rent price. Cheaper upfront, but if you can't afford rent one day you get **kicked out** automatically and lose all placed furniture arrangements.
- **Buy** — Pay the full property value once. You only pay the smaller daily maintenance cost. You can never be evicted, and you can **rent out** owned properties you're not living in.

:::tip
Renting is fine early on when cash is tight. As soon as you can afford it, **buy** your home to lock in the lower maintenance cost and open up passive income from renting.
:::

### Renting out properties

If you **own** a property but aren't living there, you can tap **Rent Out** to earn its rent price as **daily passive income**. This means owning multiple properties is a legitimate investment strategy — buy cheap, live somewhere else, and collect rent.

Tap **Evacuate** to stop renting out a property (e.g. if you want to move back in).

### Moving between properties

When you move to a different property, all your placed furniture is **unequipped** (but not lost — you still own it). You'll need to re-place furniture in your new home.

### Capacity management

Your current home's **capacity** determines how many furniture slots you have. A capacity-bar at the top of the Furniture tab shows how much space is used.

- Each furniture piece uses 1–3 slots.
- You **cannot place** a piece if it would exceed capacity.
- You can **detach** furniture to free up space (the item stays in your inventory).

:::warning
Entertainment furniture like the TV, Computer, and Game Console boost morale but **reduce health daily**. Balance them with health-boosting items like the Toilet, Shower, or Bathtub.
:::

### Furniture states

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **In Use** (green) | Currently placed in your home and providing effects |
| **Owned** (blue) | Bought but not placed — sitting in storage |
| *(no badge)* | Available for purchase |

## Theft events

There is a **1% daily chance** of a thief breaking into your home:

- If you have a **Security System** placed, the thief is caught and nothing is stolen.
- If you **don't** have one, a random piece of furniture is stolen permanently (removed from both placement and ownership).
- If you have no furniture at all, the thief finds nothing to steal.

:::tip
The Security System costs only 200 and takes 1 slot. It's one of the best investments in the game — buy it early.
:::

## Strategy guide

### Early game (low budget)

1. **Rent the Tent** (2/day) to get a home location and unlock home-based actions.
2. Buy a **Bed** (150) for daily energy recovery.
3. Save up and upgrade to the **Regular House** when you can (buy if possible for 5,200).

### Mid game (stable income)

1. **Buy** your home to stop paying rent and reduce daily costs.
2. Fill furniture slots with a balanced mix: Bed + Toilet + Portrait covers energy, health, and morale.
3. Buy the **Security System** to protect your furniture.
4. If you can afford a second property, **rent it out** for passive income.

### Late game (wealthy)

1. Upgrade to the **Mansion** or **Castle** for massive stat bonuses and furniture slots.
2. Buy and rent out multiple smaller properties for stacking passive income.
3. Fill all slots with high-value furniture — Bathtub, Computer, and Game Console pair well with health items.

---

*Property prices and effects are fixed. Furniture effects stack with property effects — the bonuses shown on your home card reflect both.*
