# Bank

The **bank** is your personal finance hub. Open a bank account and you unlock **savings**, **fixed deposits**, **loans**, **credit cards**, and a full **credit score** system. Access it from the **Assets** panel on the main screen.

## Opening an account

Tap **Assets → Bank** and press **Open Account**. Your starting **credit score** is **500** (fair). From here the bank splits into three tabs: **Overview**, **Savings**, and **Loans**.

## Overview tab

The overview shows your financial snapshot at a glance:

| Field | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Credit Score** | 300–850 gauge; determines loan eligibility and interest rates |
| **Savings Balance** | Cash sitting in your savings account, earning daily interest |
| **Total Debt** | Sum of all active loans + credit card balance |
| **Net Worth** | Savings + fixed deposits − total debt |
| **Interest Rate** | Current savings rate (fluctuates over time) |
| **Interest Earned** | Lifetime interest earned from savings and matured deposits |

You can also **hire a financial adviser** (5 gems, 30 days) from this tab — more on that below.

## Credit score

Your credit score ranges from **300** to **850** and recalculates **every 7 days** based on four factors:

| Factor | Weight | What helps |
|--------|:------:|------------|
| **Payment history** | 40% | Never miss a loan or credit card payment |
| **Debt-to-income** | 30% | Keep total debt low relative to your salary |
| **Account age** | 15% | Time since you opened the account (maxes out around 360 days) |
| **Loan diversity** | 15% | Having different loan types (up to 3 unique types) |

| Range | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 800–850 | Excellent |
| 740–799 | Very Good |
| 670–739 | Good |
| 580–669 | Fair |
| 300–579 | Poor |

:::tip
Pay every loan on time and let your account age grow — these two factors alone cover **55%** of your score.
:::

## Savings

Deposit and withdraw cash freely. Your savings balance earns **daily interest** at the current savings rate (starts around **4.5%** annually, but shifts over time with rate-change events).

- **Deposit** — moves cash from your wallet into savings.
- **Withdraw** — moves savings back to your wallet.

Interest compounds daily: the longer you leave money in, the faster it grows.

### Fixed deposits

Lock money away for a set period at a **higher rate** than regular savings. Available terms:

| Term | Rate bonus |
|------|-----------|
| **30 days** | Base fixed rate |
| **90 days** | +25% over base |
| **180 days** | +45% over base |
| **365 days** | +45% over base |

When a fixed deposit **matures**, the principal plus earned interest is automatically added to your savings balance. You **can** withdraw early, but you lose **50%** of the interest earned so far.

:::warning
Fixed deposits lock your cash — you cannot use it for rent, loans, or emergencies until the term ends (or you break it early at a penalty). Only deposit money you won't need soon.
:::

## Loans

Five loan types are available, each with different caps, rates, and requirements:

| Loan | Max amount | Rate range | Term | Min credit | Requirements |
|------|----------:|:----------:|:----:|:----------:|-------------|
| **Personal** | 10,000 | 11–22% | 60–180 days | 400 | — |
| **Auto** | 50,000 | 7–15.5% | 90–360 days | 500 | — |
| **Mortgage** | 500,000 | 4.5–10.5% | 360–720 days | 600 | Employed |
| **Student** | 20,000 | 5.5–9.5% | 120–360 days | 350 | Active education |
| **Payday** | 2,000 | 38–58% | 14–30 days | — | — |

Your actual interest rate depends on your **credit score** — higher score means lower rates. The maximum you can borrow is also capped by your **income**: the bank won't approve more than what you could repay using 50% of your daily salary.

### How repayment works

Loan payments are **deducted automatically every day** from your wallet. If you have enough cash, the payment goes through and your balance drops. You can also manually **pay extra** or **pay off** the entire remaining balance early from the Loans tab.

### What happens if you miss payments

| Missed streak | Consequence |
|:---:|-------------|
| **1** | Credit score drops **−10** per miss |
| **3 consecutive** | Debt collectors activate — warning in your diary |
| **5 consecutive** | Mortgage → **foreclosure** (lose your home). Other loans → **arrest** (14 days) |

:::warning
Payday loans have brutal rates (38–58%) and very short terms. They are a last resort — use them only when you need fast cash and can repay quickly.
:::

## Credit card

Apply for a credit card once your credit score reaches **500**. Your **limit** and **interest rate** scale with your score:

- **Limit** — ranges from ~1,000 (score 500) to ~10,000 (score 850).
- **Rate** — ranges from ~26% (low score) down to ~16% (high score).

The card accrues **daily interest** on any outstanding balance, and a small minimum payment is auto-deducted each day. If your balance exceeds **150%** of the limit, debt collectors activate.

You can make manual payments from the Loans tab to pay down the balance faster.

## Loan shark

Need cash with no credit check? The **loan shark** lends up to **5,000** — but at **58–100%** interest rates with 14–30 day terms. No credit score requirement, no questions asked.

The catch: miss **3 consecutive payments** and the loan shark's enforcers show up, dealing **−20 health**. They reset after each visit, so the beatings repeat if you keep missing.

:::warning
Loan shark debt is separate from your bank record — it doesn't affect your credit score, but it can kill you. Literally.
:::

## Bankruptcy

If your total debt becomes unmanageable, you can **file for bankruptcy** from the Overview tab. This wipes all loans and credit card debt, but:

- Credit score drops to **300**
- Bankruptcy flag lasts **360 days** — you cannot take new loans or apply for a credit card during this period
- Credit card is cancelled

You can **skip bankruptcy early** for **10 gems**, which clears the flag immediately and bumps your score back to at least **450**.

## Financial adviser (gem feature)

Hire a **financial adviser** for **5 gems** to get 30 days of improved rates:

- **Savings rate** increases by **25%**
- **Fixed deposit rates** increase by **25%**
- **Loan interest rates** decrease by **15%**

The adviser timer shows on the Overview tab. Stack it with fixed deposits for maximum returns.

## Random events

The bank is affected by events that happen as you play:

- **Interest rate changes** — savings rates can shift up or down randomly, affecting both new deposits and daily interest.
- **Identity theft** — your credit score drops **30–80 points**. You can spend **3 gems** to fix it and recover **+50** points.

## Strategy guide

### Early game

1. **Open an account** as soon as you can — account age is 15% of your credit score.
2. Deposit spare cash into **savings** for free daily interest.
3. Take a **student loan** if you're pursuing education — low rates and no job needed.

### Mid game

1. Start **fixed deposits** (90+ day terms) for better returns on money you don't need right now.
2. Apply for a **credit card** once your score hits 500 — useful as an emergency buffer.
3. Consider a **mortgage** if you want to buy a house without saving the full price upfront.

### Late game

1. Keep your credit score above **750** for the best loan terms and highest card limits.
2. Use the **financial adviser** before opening large fixed deposits to maximize interest.
3. **Never** miss payments — a single streak of 5 missed payments can cost you your home or your freedom.
