# Assets

The **Assets** panel is your personal wealth dashboard. Open it from the main game screen to manage everything you own — real estate, investments, and collectibles.

## How to open

Tap the **Assets** button on the main screen. A bottom sheet slides up showing six categories as a grid:

| Category | Status | What it does |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **House** | Available | Buy, rent, and furnish properties |
| **Stocks** | Available | Invest in companies and crypto |
| **Collections** | Available | Buy and sell collectibles at auction |
| **Vehicles** | Coming Soon | — |
| **Company** | Coming Soon | — |
| **Bank** | Coming Soon | — |

Tap any available category to expand it into a full-screen view.

## Housing

Your home determines your **max stats** (energy, morale, health), gives **daily stat bonuses**, and unlocks the **Furniture** tab where you can place items for extra effects. You can also **rent out** properties you own for passive daily income.

Full guide: [Housing](./housing.md)

## Stock market

Invest cash in **9 stocks** across 7 sectors. Prices change daily based on sector trends, random noise, and news events. Buy a mobile phone to receive news alerts.

Full guide: [Stock Market](./stock-market.md)

## Collections & auctions

Buy and sell art, antiques, coins, watches, wine, and sports memorabilia through a real-time bidding system. Collectibles appreciate over time and have hidden real values you discover through appraisals.

Full guide: [Collections & Auctions](./collections.md)

---

:::tip
Housing is the best early investment — the stat bonuses compound every single day. Once you have a stable income, branch into stocks for passive growth and collections for high-risk, high-reward profits.
:::
