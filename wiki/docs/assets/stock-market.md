# Stock market

The **stock market** lets you invest your hard-earned cash into shares of various companies. Prices change every day based on sector trends, random noise, and news events — so timing matters. You can access the stock market from the **Assets** menu (second tab).

## Getting started

Before you can trade, you need **cash**. There are no minimum account requirements — buy as few or as many shares as you can afford.

:::tip
Buy a **mobile phone** from the **Electronics Shop** to receive stock news alerts. Without one, price-moving events will happen silently and you'll miss valuable buy/sell signals.
:::

## Available stocks

There are **9 stocks** across **7 sectors**, each with different price ranges and risk levels:

| Stock | Sector | Approx. price | Risk |
|-------|--------|---------------|------|
| TechCorp | Tech | ~150 | Medium |
| ByteNet | Tech | ~80 | Medium-High |
| SolarGen | Energy | ~45 | Medium |
| PetroMax | Energy | ~120 | Low-Medium |
| MediLife | Health | ~200 | Low |
| FreshFarm | Food | ~35 | Low |
| GameStudio | Entertainment | ~60 | High |
| GoldBank | Finance | ~90 | Very Low |
| MoonCoin | Crypto | ~10 | Extreme |

**Risk** reflects how wildly the price swings day-to-day. Low-risk stocks are stable but grow slowly; high-risk stocks can double or crash quickly.

## How prices move

Each day, stock prices are influenced by:

1. **Sector trends** — entire sectors drift up or down together for a while, then reverse.
2. **Daily noise** — random fluctuations scaled by each stock's risk level.
3. **News events** — specific stories (positive or negative) push individual stocks or the whole market.
4. **Mean reversion** — prices that drift far from their base value are gently pulled back over time.

This means **no stock goes to zero forever** and **no stock rockets indefinitely**. Everything trends back toward a natural range eventually.

## Reading the market

- **Tap a stock** to expand it and see its **price chart** (look for the chevron arrow on each card).
- **Green arrow** = price went up today; **red arrow** = price went down.
- The chart shows up to **60 days** of price history — look for patterns.
- Your **Portfolio** tab shows all owned stocks with profit/loss per position.

## Buying & selling

Tap a stock to expand it, then press **Buy** or **Sell**. A prompt appears where you enter the quantity — it shows the min and max you can trade:

- **Buying:** min is 1, max is based on how much cash you have at the current share price.
- **Selling:** min is 1, max is the number of shares you own.

If you only own 1 share (or can only afford 1), the trade executes instantly without the prompt.

## News events

If you own a **mobile phone**, you'll receive news alerts in your diary **and** see a **Latest News** banner at the top of the stock market screen. Each news item shows an up/down arrow and how many days the effect lasts.

- **Positive news** (e.g. "TechCorp announces AI breakthrough") pushes a stock's price **up** for several days.
- **Negative news** (e.g. "MoonCoin exchange hacked") pushes a stock's price **down** for several days.
- **Market-wide news** (e.g. "Bull market declared") affects **all** stocks at once.

Without a mobile phone, news still affects prices — you just won't see which events are active.

:::tip
When you see negative news about a stock you don't own — that's your **buy signal**. The price will drop for a few days, then mean reversion pulls it back up. Buy the dip, sell the recovery.
:::

## Spread rumors (gem feature)

When you expand a stock card, you'll see a **Spread Rumors** button that costs **3 gems**. This injects positive buzz about the selected stock, boosting its price for the next **3 days**.

- You can only spread rumors **once every 7 days** — a cooldown timer shows when it's available again.
- Works best when you already own the stock: spread rumors, ride the price up, then sell for profit.
- Combine with buying the dip: buy a crashed stock cheaply, then spend gems to accelerate its recovery.

## Trading strategies

### Buy the dip

The safest strategy. When bad news tanks a stock below its typical range, buy shares. The built-in mean reversion will gradually push the price back up. Sell once it recovers.

### Ride the news

After positive news, a stock will climb for 2–3 days. If you already own shares, consider selling near the peak before the effect wears off.

### The MoonCoin gamble

MoonCoin has **extreme** volatility — it can surge +30% on viral hype or crash -25% on a hack. It's the cheapest stock to buy in bulk and offers the wildest swings. High risk, high reward.

### Safe parking

GoldBank and FreshFarm barely move. Use them to "park" money when you're unsure about the market — they won't lose much value while you wait for opportunities.

:::warning
Don't invest money you need for rent, education, or emergencies. Stock prices can drop and stay down for many days before recovering.
:::

## Portfolio & profit tracking

The **Portfolio** tab in the stock market shows:

- **Total portfolio value** — current market value of all your holdings.
- **Total profit/loss** — how much you've gained or lost overall.
- **Per-stock breakdown** — quantity, average cost, current value, and profit percentage for each position.

You can sell directly from the Portfolio tab using the sell button on each stock.
