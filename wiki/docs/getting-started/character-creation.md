# Character Creation

Every new life in **Life Simulator Reloaded** begins with a few hidden dice rolls and a lot of player choice afterward. This page covers **what you start with** and **how those numbers push your story**.

## Who you are

You pick identity basics (like **name**, **gender**, **country**, and **appearance**) in the app. The game also rolls **starting core stats** that matter for jobs, learning, and survival — see below.

## Starting intelligence & looks

When a new character is created, the game assigns:

| Stat | Starting range | Maximum (typical cap) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| **Intelligence** | 20–100 (random) | **200** |
| **Looks** | 20–100 (random) | **200** |

:::tip Reroll regret
You cannot “undo” bad luck on day one without starting a new game. If you care about min-maxing, know that both stats can be improved over a long life — but a strong roll still accelerates early progression.
:::

## What intelligence does

**Intelligence** influences **how fast you learn skills**. The higher your intelligence, the quicker you gain levels from courses, jobs, and practice — especially useful when you are juggling many skills at once.

## What looks does

**Looks** influences **how demanding jobs feel on paper**. Better looks slightly **ease the skill expectations** some careers have, which can open doors sooner or make promotions feel less grindy. It is not a substitute for training, but it helps alongside real skill growth.

## Money and age

| Concept | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting money** | Every new character currently begins with **50** cash (same for all runs; a future update could change this). |
| **Age & time** | You start at the **beginning of the life timeline**. **Each in-game year lasts 20 days** in real play sessions — the calendar advances as days pass. |
| **Day-by-day play** | You live **one day at a time**: actions, travel, jobs, and events consume time and move the story forward. |

:::note Why “20 days = 1 year”?
The game compresses childhood and adulthood so decades of life fit a mobile session. When this wiki says **in-game years** and **days**, it means that compressed calendar, not real-world calendar years.
:::

## Getting a good start

- **Lean into your roll:** high intelligence → prioritize **courses and skill-heavy jobs** early; high looks → you can **job-hunt aggressively** while you train gaps.
- **Plan your first job:** even a modest wage beats burning savings; use early income for **food, rest, and stability**.
- **Do not neglect “boring” stats:** health, energy, and morale still decide whether you live long enough to enjoy high intelligence or looks — see **[Basic Mechanics](./basic-mechanics)**.
- **Tutorial quests first:** they teach flows (travel, actions, decisions) and often pay **rewards** that smooth the early curve.

:::warning Stats are not everything
Random events, relationships, crime, and extra content packs can pivot a run hard. A “weak” character with good habits often outlasts a gifted one played recklessly.
:::
