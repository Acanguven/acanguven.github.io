# Beginner Tips & Common Mistakes

You do not need a perfect build to enjoy **Life Simulator Reloaded** — you need **habits** that keep your character alive long enough for the cool stuff to happen. This page collects **practical tips** and **classic mistakes** we see in community runs.

## Stay out of the danger zone

Health, energy, and morale each have a **maximum of 100** and a **danger threshold around 20**. If any bar hits **0**, you **die**.

| Tip | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep all three above 20 when possible** | Below that, you are fragile; at **0**, the run ends. |
| **Visit the hospital when health is low** | Treat early — waiting for a “free” recovery often costs more in lost days and events. |
| **Sleep, eat, and fix morale deliberately** | Energy and morale are not “nice to have”; they are **survival stats**. |

:::warning The death spiral
Players often ignore **morale** or **energy** because health still looks “okay.” One bad night plus one bad event can **zero a bar**. Check all three before risky actions.
:::

## Learn faster on purpose

- **Take courses** to raise skills that gate jobs you want — especially if your **intelligence** is average or low (every bit of learning speed helps).
- **Pick one early career target** instead of dabbling in ten skills at once; depth unlocks **income** and **new actions** sooner.

:::tip Stack bonuses
Combine **education**, **job practice**, and **safe daily routines** so skill XP is not fighting constant stat drain.
:::

## Money and gems

| Tip | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Get a job ASAP** | Even entry-level work stabilizes food, rent, and travel — **savings melt fast** without income. |
| **Do not spend every coin on vibes** | Items and impulse buys are fun, but **cash buffer** saves you when RNG taxes you. |
| **Save gems for important decisions** | Use premium currency on **high-impact** checks: brutal mini-games, quest branches you care about, or recovery moments that salvage a run. |

:::warning Spending gems on noise
Burning gems to skip **every** minor inconvenience leaves you broke when a **promotion, trial, or prison event** actually needs them.
:::

## Quests and tutorials

- **Complete tutorial quests first** — they teach navigation, actions, and systems that the rest of the game assumes you know.
- Read **quest text** when stuck: milestones often hint at **location**, **item**, or **skill** requirements.

## Relationships and social play

- **Build relationships early** — friends and partners unlock **events**, **gifts**, and **support** that pure grind cannot replace.
- **Balance social spend** with stat upkeep; a packed social calendar on **empty energy** is how accidents happen.

## Common mistakes (and how to dodge them)

| Mistake | Better habit |
| --- | --- |
| **Ignoring health until the UI screams** | Check health after **fights, illness, cheap food streaks**, and **crime** — fix problems at yellow, not red. |
| **Blowing all money on day one** | Keep a **multi-day buffer** for rent, travel, and emergencies. |
| **Never training skills** | Jobs and quests **gate** on skills; schedule **courses** like appointments. |
| **Skipping energy/morale recovery** | Treat rest and fun as **maintenance**, not optional fluff. |
| **Crime without a plan** | **Crime & Prison** content is viable — but **wanted level** and **jail time** are real progression taxes. Read **[Crime overview](../crime/overview)** before cosplaying a mastermind. |

:::tip When you lose a run
Death is data. Note **which bar hit 0** and **what you did the day before** — that pattern is usually the lesson.
:::
