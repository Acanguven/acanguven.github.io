# Social interactions

**Interactions** are per-person actions (handshake-style options in the UI). They depend on **relationship category** (family, romance, friends, coworkers, jail), your **friendship**, **love**, and **hate** with that person, and sometimes **location** or **how many times you can use them per day**.

## Core categories (recap)

| Category | Typical interactions |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | Support, gifts, working through conflict |
| **Romance** | Dates, intimacy, commitment beats |
| **Friends** | Hangouts, favors, introductions |
| **Coworkers** | Office-style socializing — can grow into real friendship |
| **Jail** | Prison-only set (needs the crime pack and an active sentence) |

Effects usually change **friendship**, **love**, **hate**, and sometimes **money**, **morale**, or **skills**.

## Prison interactions (crime pack)

You must be **in prison** and the person must be in the **jail** relationship group unless the game says otherwise.

| Interaction | Effect summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Greet** | **+3** friendship, **+2** prison reputation (limited **once per day**) |
| **Intimidate** | If **prison reputation ≥ 60**: **+3** reputation, **+10** hate and **−5** friendship on the target. Otherwise you trigger a **standard prison fight** (often **+2** days on your sentence on a typical outcome) |
| **Trade** | Needs **≥ 30** friendship; **+3** friendship, **+$5–20** |
| **Share meal** | **+10** friendship, **−5** energy |
| **Gossip** | **+5** friendship, **+3** prison life skill |
| **Ask protection** | Needs **≥ 50** friendship; **−3** friendship, **+5** prison life (up to **3** times per day) |
| **Fight** | Easier or **tougher** opponent depending on hate; win/lose follows prison fight rules |
| **Defend friend** | Needs **≥ 60** friendship; prison fight — on a win: **+20** friendship, **+5** reputation, **+2** days, plus an achievement |
| **Steal** | Success chance scales with **prison life** and **fighting** (capped); success gives money; failure costs **−20** friendship, **+20** hate, often **+2** days |
| **Extort** | Needs **prison reputation ≥ 120**; chance based on your rep vs their hate; success gives money and hate; failure often **+3** days |
| **Recruit** | Needs an **active gang** and **≥ 40** friendship; adds a member, **+15** friendship, **+3** reputation |
| **Bribe guard** | Costs **5 gems**; **50%** chance to shave **5** sentence days **or** **+30** friendship with that guard |

:::warning
Failed prison interactions often **reset your “clean record” streak** for reports and the **7-day good-behavior** check — the game tracks your last trouble day for that.
:::

## Building around interactions

- **Greet and gossip** early for safe reputation and friendship.
- **Intimidate** only when your reputation is high enough to avoid the forced brawl (**≥ 60**).
- **Extort** is late-game bullying — huge reputation requirement and a harsh failure state.

For bonds outside prison, see **[Relationships guide](./relationships-guide)** and **[Mechanics: relationships](../mechanics/relationships)**.
