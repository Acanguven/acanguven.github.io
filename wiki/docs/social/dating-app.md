# Dating app

The **dating app** is a Tinder-style feature where you swipe through profiles to find romantic matches. Open it from the **Relations** button on the top navigation bar, then switch to the **Dating App** tab.

## Requirements

Before you can start swiping, you need:

| Requirement | How to meet it |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Age 18+** | Wait until your character turns 18 |
| **Mobile phone** | Buy one from the **[Electronics Shop](../locations/electronics-shop)** |
| **Profile bio** | Write a short bio when you first open the dating app |
| **Not in prison** | You cannot use the dating app while serving a sentence |

## Setting up your profile

The first time you open the dating app, you'll create a profile:

1. **Write a bio** — type anything you like; this is your public intro.
2. **Choose your preference** — select who you're interested in: **Men**, **Women**, or **Everyone**.

Your profile also shows your character's **avatar**, **age**, **job title**, and **country** automatically. You can change your preference at any time from the profile screen.

## How swiping works

Tap **Start Swiping** to begin a session. You'll see **10 candidate profiles** — each shows a name, age, bio, and avatar. For each profile:

- **Swipe right** (or tap the heart) → you **like** them and a match roll happens behind the scenes.
- **Swipe left** (or tap the X) → you **pass** — no roll, move to the next profile.

After all 10 profiles, you'll see a **summary screen** with how many matches you got.

## Match chance

When you swipe right, the game rolls to see if it's a mutual match. Your chance depends on:

| Factor | Bonus |
|--------|:-----:|
| **Base chance** | 40% |
| **Looks stat** | +3% per level |
| **Profile boost** (gem feature) | +25% |
| **Maximum** | 95% |

Higher **looks** means more matches. If you're not matching enough, invest in your appearance — gym, fashion items, and anything that raises the looks stat.

:::tip
A character with **looks level 10** has a base match chance of **70%** — and with a boost, that jumps to **95%**. Looks matter on dating apps, even simulated ones.
:::

## What happens on a match

When you match with someone:

- They're added to your **Known People** list as a **romantic contact**.
- A diary entry confirms the match.
- The **dating app matches** statistic increments.

From there, you can interact with them like any other romantic contact — dates, gifts, and relationship-building through the [social system](./relationships-guide).

## Cooldown

After completing a swipe session, there's a **3-day cooldown** before you can swipe again. The remaining days are shown on the dating app screen.

Don't want to wait? You can **reset the cooldown** for **5 gems**.

## Profile boost (gem feature)

Before starting a swipe session, you can **boost your profile** for **3 gems**. This adds **+25%** to your match chance for the entire session (all 10 swipes). The boost badge appears on your profile while active.

Best used when your looks stat is already decent — stacking a high base with the boost gets you close to the **95%** cap.

## Incoming matches

Even when you're not actively swiping, the dating app works in the background. Each day there's a **5% chance** that someone likes your profile and becomes a match automatically. When this happens, you'll see a diary notification.

This passive feature requires the same eligibility — age 18+, mobile phone, profile set up, and not in prison.

## Achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock |
|-------------|---------------|
| **First Dating Match** | Get your first match (swiping or incoming) |

## Strategy tips

### Maximize your match rate

1. **Level up looks** before heavy swiping sessions — gym, fashion, and related items all help.
2. **Boost** when your looks are already high to push near the 95% cap.
3. Don't bother swiping if your looks stat is very low — invest first, swipe later.

### Build your social circle

The dating app is the fastest way to add romantic NPCs to your relations list. Combine it with:

- **Park and restaurant dates** to grow love and friendship with matches.
- **Gift-giving** to accelerate relationship meters.
- **Marriage** once a relationship is strong enough.

### Gem efficiency

- **3 gems for a boost** is worth it when your looks are moderate (level 5–8) — the +25% makes a big difference in that range.
- **5 gems for cooldown reset** is useful if you're actively trying to build a romantic social network quickly.
- If your looks are already at level 10+, skip the boost and save your gems — you're hitting near-cap anyway.
