# Relationships guide

Every NPC has **friendship**, **love**, and **hate** (and other flags) grouped by relationship **type** (family, romance, friends, work, jail, and so on). Gameplay is about **moving those numbers** over time — not a single branching dialogue tree.

## Building and maintaining bonds

| Lever | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Consistency** | Interact **daily** when limits allow; many actions are capped per day |
| **Alignment** | Pick **story choices** that fit the situation (work vs home, and so on) |
| **Gifts and activities** | Use location actions (parks, restaurants) that explicitly help relationships |
| **Avoid hate spikes** | High **hate** unlocks sabotage and fights; recover with apologies or space |

:::tip
**Coworkers** can become **friends** after enough positive socializing — great for job stability and social invites.
:::

## Family

Family ties help **early stability**:

- They respond to **support** interactions and shared events
- **Letters from prison** can randomly raise a family member’s **friendship** (+5) when you write from a cell or library
- Deaths, divorces, and rivalries still come from **base-game random events**

Keep **morale** up so you have energy to maintain family during grindy jobs.

## Romance

Romantic arcs use the **romance** category:

1. Raise **love** and **friendship** through flirt and date style beats (exact options vary by age and location).
2. Watch for **blockers** — existing partners, age limits, or conflicting story situations.
3. When **commitment** choices appear, decide carefully; saying no often has a cost.

:::info
Love and friendship are **separate** meters. You can be close friends without romance, or romantic while fixing hate after a fight — know which bar you are working on.
:::

## Marriage (practical)

- **Stabilize income** first — weddings and housing strain money.
- **Raise friendship** before proposals if the game checks trust; some beats fail if you rush.
- After marriage, **keep showing up** with positive interactions; neglect can push **hate** up in some event pools.

:::warning
Big negative swings (cheating beats, crime coming to light) can crash several meters at once. Keep **gems** in mind for damage-control choices if you play risky.
:::
