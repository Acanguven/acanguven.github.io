# Course list

Player-facing catalog aligned to careers.

**Specialist electives (any path):** leadership, public speaking, self defense, financial literacy, cooking masterclass, survival basics, digital marketing, meditation — patch weak skills for promotions or risky events.

:::tip
**Public speaking** is the best multi-career buy if you rotate through waitress, bartender, or actor.
:::

## Courses by career (unlock order)

| Career | Course chain | Unlocks / powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School | Math, reading, science, sports, debate, first aid, and related school subjects | Academics + teen job readiness |
| Beggar | Street survival → persuasion workshop | Street + persuasion gates |
| Bartender | Mixology course | **Master bartender** promotion |
| Waitress | Customer service | Communication floor |
| Cook | Cooking techniques → gourmet | Kitchen ladder + high ceiling |
| Developer | Software apprenticeship → software university | Programming ranks |
| Banker | Economics app → economics college | Finance ladder |
| Delivery | Driving license → secure driving | Commercial driving tiers |
| Actor | Acting starter → professional acting | Background+ roles (celebrity) |

## Suggested purchase order

1. **Driving license** if you need quick employable income.
2. **One** apprenticeship matching your chosen job (mixology, cook, software, economics, acting).
3. Matching **college** course right before the promotion that lists it.
4. Specialist electives only to fix skills you fail in **decisions** or **interviews**.

:::tip
Do not enroll in **gourmet** or **secure driving** until the prerequisite course is **fully** done — partial progress never satisfies a promotion’s **required course**.
:::
