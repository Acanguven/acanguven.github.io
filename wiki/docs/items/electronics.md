# Electronics

**Electronics** cover **phones**, **computers**, **radios**, and gadgets that unlock **connectivity**, **career actions**, or **crime utilities**. Many are **passive** (carry to enable features); some are **usable** with charges or risk.

## Phones

Regular and **specialty phones** may enable **calls**, **social** shortcuts, or **crime** perks (for example, **untraceable** or **smuggled** variants tied to risk). **Prison phones** are powerful but can trigger **searches**, **confiscation**, or **extra sentence**—treat them as high-stakes tools, not toys.

## Computers and internet

**Computers** (often paired with **internet** effects) unlock **developer** work, **computer skill** training, and digital side content. Buy them when you commit to **tech careers**; they are poor impulse purchases for a purely manual job path.

### Gaming PC

The **gaming PC** ($2,500) is a premium computer available at the **electronics shop**. It provides internet access like a laptop but also **boosts streaming performance** — higher viewer counts, better earnings, and an easier **Go Live** mini-game.

You can **upgrade** the gaming PC up to **level 5** at the electronics shop. Each level costs the current level × $500 and improves streaming bonuses further. Maxing it out unlocks the **Ultimate Setup** achievement.

:::tip
If you plan to pursue the **streamer** career, invest in a gaming PC early. The performance difference over a laptop is significant and each upgrade pays for itself quickly through better stream earnings.
:::

## Other gear

**Radios**, **police scanners**, and similar items skew toward **morale**, **information**, or **evasion** bonuses. Expect trade-offs—confiscation chances in restricted areas, decay, or ongoing money costs. When an item description mentions **easier escape** or **witness reduction**, it is usually aimed at **crime** arcs rather than everyday convenience.

:::tip
Align electronics with your **job**: developers want a stable **computer + internet** stack; criminals may prioritize **black-market** phones. Do not carry **smuggled prison phones** during heavy **shakedown** weeks unless you have a **stash** or accept the sentence risk.
:::
