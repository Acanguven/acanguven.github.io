# Weapons

**Weapons** let you fight **NPCs**, survive **prison**, and (with the Monster Hunter module) **hunt** supernatural enemies. They range from **improvised shivs** to **firearms** with **ammo** economies.

## Guns and ammo

**Rifles** and **handguns** (where available) consume **ammo** per use. In hunts, rifles can **rarely break**—budget **replacement cost** and keep spare ammo before long trips. Buy ammo at the **gun shop** or earn it from **drops**.

## Melee and tools

**Knives**, **chainsaws**, **silver daggers**, and **prison shivs** fill melee niches. Prison gear is tuned for **inmate fights**; hunting daggers add **type bonuses** (e.g. skeletons). Shivs are often **crafted** rather than purchased. Always verify an attack is available in your current **fight** UI—missing ammo or a broken gun silently removes options.

## Legal vs illegal carry

Using weapons in the wrong context raises **crime**, **heat**, or **search** risk. Store or sell contraband before predictable checks when the story allows.

:::tip
For **monster hunting**, bring **both** ranged and melee options: rifles for raw damage, daggers/chainsaws for skill training and type matchups. For **prison**, a **shiv** or **knife** wins fights faster than bare fists—pair with **fighting** skill grinding.
:::
