# Prison items

Prison items come from the **prison market** (canteen), **crafting**, **gems**, or **fight rewards**. They manage **meters**, **reputation**, **fights**, and **escape** attempts. Several are **contraband**: guards can **shake you down**; failing the hide minigame costs items and adds **sentence**.

## Canteen staples (money)

| Item | Price | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Prison water | 1 | Cheap health |
| Prison toast / chocolate | 2 | Morale / mixed meters |
| Prison ramen / coffee | 3 | Solid all-around snack or energy |
| Cigarettes | 5 | Morale up, small health cost |
| Softcore magazine | 30 | Big morale read |
| Prison diary | 10 | Morale + **prison life** on use (one per prisoner) |
| Workout band | 25 | Boosts prison workout action |
| Prison weights | 50 | Passive **sports** XP daily (one per prisoner) |
| Prison radio | 40 | Morale/energy listen; **2%/day** confiscation risk |
| Tattoo kit | 80 | Rep + **prison life**; hurts health, +1 day sentence |
| Contraband stash | 120 | Cuts shakedown losses (**50%** skip when proccing) |
| Prison knife | 120 | Strong fight weapon (one per prisoner) |
| Escape blueprint | 200 | **Extra escape attempt** in the prison escape flow |

## Gems and crafted gear

- **Smuggled phone** — **10 gems** (no cash price). Enables outside calls; **~3%/day** confiscation roll that **adds 5 days** if caught.
- **Shiv** — **not sold**; **craft** in prison (achievement **Craftsman**). Weaker than the bought knife but free if you have the route.

## Fight rewards (no shop price)

- **Champion belt** — earned by beating the **champion** after **5 prison fight wins** (one-time event). Passively grants **+3 prison reputation per day**.
- **Guard uniform** — helps **escape** attempts; treated as **contraband** like shiv/phone during shakedowns. It is **not** on the standard canteen list—watch prison events and content updates for how yours appears.

:::tip
**First purchases:** **ramen/coffee** for sustain, **diary** for **prison life**, then **stash** if you run contraband. Save **blueprint** for when you are actively escaping. Delay the **gem phone** until you accept sentence risk or have **stash** protection.
:::
