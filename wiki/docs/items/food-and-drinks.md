# Food & drinks

**Food** and **drinks** are the most common **consumables**: they patch your **status meters** after work, travel, fights, or illness. They are sold at the **market**, **restaurant**, **bar**, and module-specific vendors (e.g. **prison** canteen items).

## What they restore

| Typical effect | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Health** | Recovery after damage, disease, or risky actions |
| **Energy** | Fuel for daily actions; running empty blocks productivity |
| **Morale** | Mental stability; at **0** morale you can die like health/energy |

Some items skew heavily toward one meter (snacks for morale, water for health); **combo meals** spread smaller gains across two or three meters. Read each item’s effect before eating during a crisis—you might need morale more than health.

## Prison and specialty food

The **crime** module adds **prison rations**-style items (toast, ramen, chocolate, coffee) tuned for cheap meter recovery behind bars. They are ideal for stretching a sentence without gem spending.

:::tip
Keep meters **above 20** when possible—that band avoids many “danger” outcomes. Carry **one morale snack** even on money-tight runs; morale crashes end runs as surely as health drops.
:::

---

*Alcohol or risky consumables may trade short-term morale for long-term penalties—check logs and effects.*
