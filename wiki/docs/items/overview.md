# Items overview

**Life Simulator Reloaded** uses **items** for survival (food, medicine), progression (books, training tools), combat (weapons, ammo), careers (electronics, uniforms), and module-specific fantasy (prison contraband, hunting gear, mystery rewards).

## Categories

Items are grouped in the UI by **category labels** (food, drinks, weapons, electronics, books, tools, drugs, hunting, etc.). Categories help you browse the **market** and understand **effects** at a glance—always read the effect text before bulk-buying.

## Market system

Most legal goods appear at the **market**, **specialty shops** (gun shop, electronics, hospital, Hunter’s Guild), or **job-related** vendors. Some items are **prison-only**, **black market**, or **gem-priced**. Prices and availability can depend on **location**, **privileges**, and whether you already own a unique piece (one-per-character flags).

## Using items

**Usable** items are spent or triggered from inventory: they may restore **health**, **energy**, or **morale**, grant **skill** or **stat** progress, or start interactions (phone calls, quests). **Passive** items apply while carried—training gear, trackers, reputation belts. A few items risk **confiscation** or **breakage**; see **[Prison items](./prison-items)** and **[Hunting items](./hunting-items)**. **Gems** can shortcut some purchases; treat them as emergency budget, not your primary grocery money.

:::tip
Before long trips or crime sprees, fill **cheap stackables** (food, drinks) and one **emergency** consumable. Unique tools (blueprints, trackers) are better bought once at the right location than panic-purchased mid-crisis.
:::
