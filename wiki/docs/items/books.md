# Books

**Books** and readable items are a steady path to **intelligence**, **skill ticks**, and **morale** swings. They appear in the **market**, **library**, and special vendors; some are **single-use** while others are flavor logs with stat effects.

## Intelligence and learning

Many books push **intelligence** or **academics**—ideal for students and **white-collar** careers. Stack reading with **school** and **courses** for faster promotion gates.

## Skill training

Skill-specific manuals (or generic “improvement” reads) grant **targeted skill** progress. Match the book to your planned job: **cooking** tomes for chefs, **crime** manuals for illegal playstyles (where offered). Public library visits and market restocks rotate what is easy to buy—check both when min-maxing.

## Prison reading

Prison adds **morale** reads (magazines, **diary** writing) and **prison life** training through written routines. The **prison scholar** achievement rewards dedicated study behind bars—pair books with safe yard days.

:::tip
Read during **low-risk** days when energy is cheap—books rarely outperform a paid work shift for **money**, but they **compound** across decades. In prison, the **diary** mixes morale and **prison life** for efficient sentence use.
:::
