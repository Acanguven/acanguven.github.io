Game modules

Life Simulator Reloaded includes a core experience plus three optional content packs. Each pack adds locations, jobs, items, quests, and mechanics into the same world. You buy or unlock them in the modules store, and they become part of your save.

The four modules​

ModuleWhat it adds
CoreBase city, careers, education, health, relationships, housing — the full default game
CrimeIllegal actions, wanted level and prison, life inside jail, prison fights and special items
CelebrityFame-driven careers, acting and spotlight-style content
Monster HunterHunter’s guild, dark forest hunting, tracker gear, monster fights and crafting
info

Only core is always available. Crime, Celebrity, and Monster Hunter are optional. If a guide mentions prison or monsters, that content needs the matching pack.

How it fits together​

Together, the packs define:

  • City and travel — where you can go and how places connect
  • Actions — what you can do at each location
  • Shops and schools — what you can buy or study
  • Quests and achievements — extra goals and rewards
tipp

Pick packs that match how you want to play: Crime for outlaw runs, Celebrity for fame and performance careers, Monster Hunter for hunting and combat-loot loops. You can use several in one long life.
